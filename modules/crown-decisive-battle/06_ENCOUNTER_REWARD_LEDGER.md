## 6.1 Method
- **Formal EL** comes from DMG v3.5 Table 3–1, Encounter Numbers (citation as verified on the Bhaal 06 ledger: printed p. 49). Staging, morale and escape never lower the formal label.
- **Battles of 100–5,000** run on GURPS Mass Combat with a **recorded live result** (D5; C14). They carry **no EL**. Personal fights inside a battle are **significant actions** run on the fused engine.
- **Class-levelled NPCs:** CR equals class level (DMG NPC rule, S). A member kitted above NPC gear value (a Stride suit, a T1 signature item) is flagged **CR +1 (I)** for planning. The formal label uses the plain level.
- **XP.**
	- Jörmun is Sorcerer 20 (Black Road character register; S).
	- Per-creature XP comes from DMG Table 2–6 at level 20 and is computed at fire from the actual defeated CRs. **SR:** the Table 2–6 values at level 20 are not transcribed here.
	- **Mass Combat XP is a CAMPAIGN-RULING the module needs from Chad.** Proposal: no per-head XP. The battle pays one objective award by outcome (6.3).
- **Allies are not the party.** Jin, the Hand, Virelle, Gary, Mago and the wedge are NPC allies. Their kills pay Jörmun nothing unless the objective rule says so.

## 6.3 Objective awards (proposal for Chad's XP ruling)
- **OA-1 The Crown holds and the bank is intact.** Single award, by outcome band: full win / held with the bank lost / held at the cost of the garrison.
- **OA-2 The wedge closes the trap** (Zama achieved).
- **OA-3 A command prize taken alive** (Vraxis, a Red Wizard commander, or the answer to the cold). This pays once, whoever takes custody.
- **Non-duplication:** E3's battle pays only through OA-1 and OA-2. E4–E6 pay per-creature XP only for foes Jörmun personally overcomes.
