**Default (I):** inside his region the Legate holds sovereign authority, so prisoners and finds taken on Crown ground are **Jörmun's to assign**. He may:

- **Let Jin and Virelle contest it.** A GURPS Quick Contest of their best relevant skills from their sheets (Virelle's port is still pending), or a negotiated split.

## 7.7 Failure continuation

- **Commander escapes:** Thay learns the Crown's true strength, including any layers it revealed, through the Enemy Knowledge update.

- None of these dead-ends the arc. The parley, Hollow Reach and the Cult thread all continue with reduced leverage.

## 7.9 The interrogation procedure (hybrid-information-gathering; both systems)
- **Unwilling subject (3.5e):** the interrogator's Intimidate is opposed by the subject's Will save. **GURPS:** a Quick Contest of Interrogation vs Will.

	| Margin | Result |
	|---|---|
	| 10+ | Full cooperation; Tier 3 answers |
	| 5–9 | Mostly truthful; Tier 2 |
	| 1–4 | Partial; Tier 1 only, and may omit |
	| Tie | Stonewalls |
	| Loss | Defiance or false information. The GM gives the listed **lie** |

- **Willing or parleying subject:** Diplomacy vs Will. Attitude modifier: friendly +4, indifferent 0, unfriendly −4, hostile −8.
- **Modifiers:**
	- physical duress +2 to +4, but 50% of answers are false and it carries a reputation cost
	- *zone of truth* or equivalent: no lies, but the subject may stay silent
	- psychological pressure +2, needs hours of setup
	- leverage +4 to +8, creates an enemy
- **Time:** quick questions take minutes; a detailed debrief takes hours; a full extraction takes days. One full extraction per prisoner per day.
- **The Hand and the Veil** bring their own interrogators: Virelle's operatives and Jin's priests. Whoever holds custody runs the extraction, and what they learn they share only by choice.
- **Tiers:** every prisoner below has Tier 1, 2 and 3 facts, plus a ready **lie** for a failed contest.

## 7.11 The documents (prop text)

**C-2(a) — The column orders**, under the tharchion's seal:
> *By the hand of Vraxis Mor-Sallar, Tharchion of the Northern Watch, to the commander of the column.*
> *One. Your object is the storm-bank at the springs men call the Crown: the cells, the racks, and the bays where the cartmen charge their wagons. Take them whole. A bank that bleeds its charge is worth nothing to us, and they will try to empty it at you.*
> *Two. The serpent will fight. He cannot stand among his own soldiers; our watchers at the boundary saw them keep their distance from him. Make him choose between his wall and his bank, and he will choose wrong once.*
> *Three. The wagons are their only quick arm. Send the lancers south first. Strike at the springs only when the wagons are past the hour they can return.*
> *Four. The red has not been seen over the Crown since the spring. The seers count it gone.*
> *Five. The bank is fed by kites flown on wires into the lightning. Cut the wires before you close.*
> *Six. Raise as you go, but the ice there is said to keep what dies. Test it on our own fallen before you trust it. If the bank cannot be taken whole, withdraw. Do not burn what the Zulkirs want.*

The two lines about Gary and the aura are **dramatic irony**: point four is exactly the blind spot the Isle de Troll ruling built.

**C-2(a) annex — The scrying record.** The seers of the Northern Watch first marked the Array by **lightning drawn down onto the sonde field at 400 ft over the springs**. Each sonde strike is visible from scrying range. That is how a detection skill of 20 found it. **Counter:** fly the sondes only under concealment, or accept being seen. This is a lever for the Veil.

**C-2(b) — Vraxis's spellbook, the ward pages.** These are the working formulae of the anti-aerial lattice over Vraxhal's inner keep (canon radius 800 ft from the central spire).
- **R (design):** holding and studying them for one week gives Jörmun **+4 on checks to dispel, bypass or detect those wards**. A full month's study by Seliara or an arcane specialist lets the empire **reproduce** the ward. That is a defence for its own dragons' enemies, and a Chekhov item.

**C-2(c) — Timetables** (S, Akhet-Senet and Vraxis pages): surge of 5–7k within 10 days of the south-Throat fire; relief of 8–12k in 30–40 days.

**S-1 — The Delegation archive**, one live variant (the mandate is set when Chad rules or rolls it; text for all five):
- *Non-aggression pact:* a draft treaty fixing the Thay–Legate border at the Sun-Crowned Heights foothills, unsigned. The battle makes it a lie.
- *Transaction:* a price list. Charge sold to Thay by the barrel, and a bid for Seliara's keying method.
- *Recruitment probe:* letters weighing whether Jörmun can be turned from Arik, with a list of what each Zulkir would offer him.
- *Intelligence pretext:* the parley's real product. Sketches of the Crown's wall, the ice wall and the spring mouths, and a count of the garrison: "about thirty".
- *Ultimatum:* a sealed demand to vacate the springs by the next new moon or "be made to". The battle is its delivery.

**S-2 — The paymaster's ledger** (Zhent layer):
> *Paid to W. Kellerhand, captain: for four hundred swords, one tenday's hire and the second half on the bank's taking. Paid via the Black Road factor at the dry well. Further: one chest to the factor, "for the convoy schedules".*

That proves Thay–Zhent coordination and points straight at the Black Road convoys.

**S-3 — The lancer route card.** A hand-drawn line from the plateau south around the Rest, then 37 miles south of the Crown to "the second bank — eight men, water carted weekly".
- *Branch A (scrying):* margin note: "seen by the kite-lightning, second field". Same source as C-2's annex.

## 7.12 The answer to the cold, all three forms (F8)
**(a) The Hearthward** (F8 3–8): a warding rite.
- Four basalt stones, each carved with a brazier sigil, set in a 100-ft square and fed by two casters.
- **R (design):** inside the square, Jörmun's Cold Aura damage is **halved** and its movement penalty is suppressed. Thay's troops can hold ground he would otherwise freeze them out of.
- Each stone has hardness 8 and 60 hp. Lose one and the ward fails.
- **Keeper:** the senior ward-caster, a Red Wizard of Abjuration (Wizard 12, Will +9).
- **T1:** how many stones. **T2:** who designed it (Vraxhal's ward-school).

**(b) The Cold-Breakers** (F8 9–13): a cadre of 8 Red Wizards drilled against Jörmun. Every spell they use is SRD.
- Their doctrine is in this order:
	1. *Resist energy (cold)* and *protection from energy* on the line.
	2. A volley of cheap save-or-suffer spells to **strip his Legendary Resistance (3/day)**.
	3. *Dimensional anchor* to pin him to the ground he chose.
	4. *Forcecage* on the anchored lindwurm.
	5. Fire for the finish.
- **Keeper:** the cadre's drill-master, Wizard 14 (Will +10). He holds the written doctrine, which is the capture prize.
- **T1:** the volley order. **T2:** how many cadres Thay is training.

**(c) The Akhet Brazier** (F8 14–18): an **artifact candidate**, which goes to the Artifacts DB and needs Chad's approval.
- A bronze brazier from the Sun-Crowned Heights, its bowl holding a coal that has burned since the Mulhorandi founding of the plateau's temples.
- **R (design, artifact tier):** within 60 ft of it, cold damage of every kind is converted to fire. Jörmun's aura there burns his enemies' allies' way instead, and his breath's cold half becomes fire.
- It is a hard counter. It needs a bearer, it can be **extinguished by smothering it in snow from the Frozen Threshold**, and it is exactly the object Jin's trigger names.
- **Keeper:** its bearer, a Red Wizard of Evocation (Wizard 14, Will +10).
- **T1:** its range. **T2:** the smothering weakness. **T3:** that a second brazier exists at Vraxhal.
