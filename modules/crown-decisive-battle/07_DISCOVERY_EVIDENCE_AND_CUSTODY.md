## 7.6 Custody
**Default (I):** inside his region the Legate holds sovereign authority, so prisoners and finds taken on Crown ground are **Jörmun's to assign**. He may:
- **Rule custody himself.** Fast, but it costs standing with whichever of Jin or Virelle loses, and Jin answers to the Emperor.
- **Let Jin and Virelle contest it.** A GURPS Quick Contest of their best relevant skills from their sheets (Virelle's port is still pending), or a negotiated split.
- **Keep the prize under Crown seal** pending Arik. This buys time and freezes both.

## 7.7 Failure continuation
- **Every centrepiece missed:** Jörmun still knows Thay came to seize the bank. He lacks proof, the ward grammar and the counter-intelligence. Hollow Reach goes ahead blind, and Arik hears Thay's version first.
- **Commander escapes:** Thay learns the Crown's true strength, including any layers it revealed, through the Enemy Knowledge update.
- **Custody fight lost to Jin:** dangerous items are destroyed or taken to the throne, and the proof goes with them.
- **Custody fight lost to Virelle:** the proof is held by the Veil and rationed to Jörmun.
- None of these dead-ends the arc. The parley, Hollow Reach and the Cult thread all continue with reduced leverage.

## 7.8 Canon-sensitive options (Chad)
- **Questioning the dead in the ice.** Thayans who die inside the 10-mile territory are trapped in Jörmun's ice (Frozen Threshold). Whether Jörmun can interrogate them is not in canon. If he can, every Thayan dead is a prisoner who cannot be ransomed or rescued. That is a huge intelligence engine, and it is grim.
- **The leak behind S-3.** Thay's scrying, or a mole: a Bedine, a Legion soldier, or someone at the Crown. The Veil has infiltrators in all three populations (5 Oct ruling). A real mole would be a new thread.
