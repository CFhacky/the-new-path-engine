## 03 — Design brief (for Gate B)

**Player-facing objective.** Hold the Crown and its charge banks against a Thayan move to seize them. Do it without breaking the Legate mandate, and without Jörmun's own cold destroying what he is defending.

**Concealed truth: "Zama in reverse".**
- At Zama, Rome's cavalry was drawn off the field and came back at the decisive moment to hit Hannibal's rear.
- Here the bait is aimed at **the defender's cavalry**. Thay knows from detection that the charge site exists and that the wedge is its fast arm.
- Thay opens with a raid toward the region's edge or on Node Two, built to pull Gaius Cuneus into pursuit. That plays on his Overconfidence and the core clock.
- The main blow then falls on the Crown, while the wedge is out past its turn-back point.
- The battle turns on whether the wedge comes back in time. That depends on Jörmun's orders, the pursuit roll, the clock, the storm and Node Two's charge. If it does come back, the Zama manoeuvre happens to Thay instead.

**Opposition objective.**
- Thay's aim is to seize the bank and the bays intact (Annex: "on 16+, Thay seizes").
- That gives Thay a power source that works under Jörmun's air threat, and proof that the lindwurm can be held off by leverage. Thay's leverage options are numbers, a captured asset, and a magical edge against the cold (C6).
- The **Thay option** is rolled when the module triggers:
	- Zaltor's delegation turns the parley into a decapitation cover
	- a Vraxis column
	- both together
	- an Arcane Brotherhood-backed mixed force

**Why now.** Thay rolled a seize intent on its monthly detection roll, after the Crown node (Phase A) exists. This module is how that result plays out.

**Success.**
- The Crown holds and the bank is intact.
- The wedge closes the trap from the rear.
- Thayan dead fall under the Frozen Threshold.
- Thay loses face along the border and may sue at the parley.

**Partial success.**
- The Crown holds, but the bank dumps or is breached.
- Or the wedge is cut off, and Node Two falls or is abandoned.
- Or Jörmun saves the Crown at the cost of his own garrison, because the aura cannot fight beside them.

**Failure continuation.**
- Thay takes the bank and withdraws with charged cells, or holds the site.
- Node Two is lost.
- The Hollow Reach arc and the Legate mandate come under pressure, since retaking means engaging Thay beyond a defensive posture.
- Play continues as a counter-raid or a negotiation.

**Character relevance.**
- **Jörmun:** the aura constraint is the dilemma. He cannot stand on the wall beside Mago's ~30, and Gary is vulnerable to cold, so he must fight apart from both. Thay's "magical edge against the cold" option tests him directly.
- **Gary:** a catastrophe if pointed well and useless if left to improvise. Sania's handling decides it.
- **Cuneus:** the bait is built for him. The cost to Cuneus is his pursuit roll, his clock, and possibly his life or command.
- **Mago:** holds the anvil with ~30.

**Campaign changes.** These depend on the result:
- the Crown phase track (Security and Investment)
- the Legate mandate (pursuit into Thay needs Jörmun's order and renegotiation)
- the Hollow Reach arc
- the Zaltor parley
- the Cult and Shade Remnant reading of Thay's weakness

**Session architecture.**
1. **The bait.** The raid and Cuneus's pursuit decision come first, then Jörmun's orders.
2. **The blow on the Crown.** Mass Combat with a recorded live result. Personal fights, including Jörmun's and Gary's, run on the fused engine. Pause points follow C13.
3. **Aftermath.** Frozen Threshold souls, prisoners, the bank's state, and the parley.

**Rolls held until after Gate B** (campaign convention):
- the plateau's distance to the Crown (C9)
- the Thay option table and the size of the committed force
- the bait's form (edge raid or Node Two)
