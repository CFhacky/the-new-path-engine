## 8.1 Session 1 — "Lancers in the South"
**Opening position.** Day 1, dawn, at the Crown.
- Jörmun is at the Crown.
- Mago's ~30 are on the walls, less the 8 at Warden's Vigil, so about 22 at the Crown.
- Cuneus's wedge is at the Crown, or on its weekly water run to Node Two (roll a d6: 1–2 on the run).
- Node Two holds its 8.
- Gary and Sania are at Isle de Troll, Gary cat-sized in Sania's coat.
- The sondes are flying.
- The Thayan parley party is at the boundary if F1 includes the Delegation.

**The player's objective:** learn what is coming, from which direction and how big, and decide what to commit before Thay decides for him.
**Thay's objective:** pull the wedge south (the bait) and keep the main body unseen as long as it can.

**Locations:** the Crown and the Vigil (04 §2); Node Two (37 mi S); the Al-Saif camps (southern flank); the plateau approaches (04 §3).

**Encounter sequence (situations, not a script):**
1. **Scouting window opens.** For each Thayan hidden layer, the empire rolls scouting with its best available scout (05 §4). The main body is visible once it moves: the Vigil sees it on the NE route, Khorzad on the others.
	- Each success is a report scene: Khorzad landing on his outcrop, the Vigil's brazier, an Al-Rashid rider.
	- Each critical failure is a false report (F12).
2. **E1, the bait strikes** (F3, F4) on Day 1 or 2 (8.4).
	- A runner, a crystal call from Node Two, or Al-Saif riders bring it in.
	- **Decision point:** send the wedge, hold it, or send part of it (a Wardrake and the Roadrider).
	- Cuneus wants to go. *Gaius Cuneus's Overconfidence makes the case for him.*
3. **Calls Jörmun can make, each a clock:**
	- **Gary and Sania:** a crystal call to Isle de Troll; arrival in 24–36 h (04 errata).
	- **Bedine riders:** a request to Farouk (Al-Rashid, blood-oath) or Al-Saif. Size and arrival by F11. Spirit-speaker politics apply.
	- **Korgan:** asking for more Legion reaches past the module window (05 §3).
	- **The Veil:** Jörmun can ask Virelle, but cannot command her. Her trigger decides (05 §9).
4. **E2, the Vigil sighting** (F5 3–8): the brazier signal, and the watch's withdrawal through the passage.
5. **If the Delegation is at the boundary:** Zaltor's parley becomes a scene. It could be a mask (F1 3–6 or 12–15) or genuine. S-1's live variant tells the GM which.

**Response branches:**
- **The wedge goes south:** run E1 on the fused engine or Mass Combat per its size, then **Cuneus's pursuit** (06 §6.2 E1).
	- Turn-back radius: 30 mi on the core pair, 45 mi with the stowed core.
	- Out of contact while the lancers withdraw: 3d6 vs 12. On a failure he pursues one more leg.
	- **If the Node Two bay is wrecked,** the wedge is on stowed cores, 37 mi out.
- **The wedge holds:** the bait becomes a real raid. Node Two's 8 fight alone, and the Al-Saif camps take losses, which costs Al-Saif standing. The main body still comes, and it now knows the wedge is home.
- **Gary called on Day 1:** he arrives Day 2–3, in time if the blow lands on Day 3 or later (8.4).

**Discoveries live here:** S-3 (route card) and P8 (Captain Sulk), if E1 is won and he is taken; S-9 (signal codebook); S-13 (Vigil log).

**Trackers changed:** wedge core clock · Node Two status · Al-Saif standing · calls made, with their arrival clocks · scouting results (what the player believes) · hidden layers revealed so far.

**Partial success:** the bait is repulsed but the wedge is drawn past its turn-back radius, or Node Two is lost.
**Failure continuation:** Node Two falls and its 8 are taken (R-4). The wedge is stranded. Session 2 opens with Thay at the Crown and no fast arm.
**End state, then transition:** the main body's dust on the northern horizon, or its silence, if it took the west route unseen. **Cut** to the night before the blow.

## 8.2 Session 2 — "The Blow on the Crown"
**Opening position.** The night before contact, about 8 h out.
- The main body is at its approach (F5).
- The garrison is on the walls.
- The ice wall's fighting platform is held without Jörmun. **The aura rule** (04 §4) means he fights forward, in the kill zone or in the air.
- Khorzad is overhead.
- Vorian's screen sits at the gate. Vorian's sealed suit can go into the aura for short spells; Ersk and Safiya cannot.

**The player's objective:** hold the Crown and the bank. Kill or take the commander. Survive the answer to the cold.
**Thay's objective:** take the bank and bays intact (seize intent). If that fails, withdraw. Orders forbid burning it (C-2a).

**Encounter sequence:**
1. **Contact. Mass Combat round 1 (E3),** with a **recorded live GURPS result**.
	- TS from 05 §1. Thay: the column F2, undead F13 at 3d6+3 quality, the bait remnant, and any revealed layers.
	- Empire: the garrison, Khorzad, the screen, plus anything that has arrived.
	- Fortification enters once, through the holding card (SR). Until it is written, the live result stands.
2. **Significant actions inside the battle:**
	- **E4:** Jörmun against the commander (F17).
	- **E5:** the answer to the cold, if F7 rolled it (7.12).
	- **E6:** the decapitation strike on the command, if F1 rolled it.
	- **E8:** a Tariq parley, if the Al-Asad layer reveals.
	- **E9:** the Thayan raising contest, every round that Thay raises its dead inside the Threshold.
3. **Pause points:** each unrevealed layer's arrival (F9), Gary's arrival (E7), the wedge's return, a named death or capture, a mass surrender, and the third side (the Cult) entering. **At each pause, recompute Mass Combat and record the new live result.**
4. **E11, the bank.** If the line breaks at the bank, Jörmun or Mago can order a deliberate dump (1d6 per stored charge; 30-ft radius per 25 charges). It denies Thay the prize and costs the charge. The 350-ft standoff protects the springs.
5. **The wedge's return** is the Zama moment.
	- If the wedge comes back into Thay's rear, the Mass Combat result recomputes with it as a flank force.
	- If it doesn't, Mago holds the anvil alone.

**Response branches:**
- **Answer to the cold present and unbroken:** Jörmun's best tool is blunted (Hearthward), countered (Cold-Breakers) or turned (Brazier).
	- Gary is cold-vulnerable, so he becomes the only ally who can stand close under the Brazier, because he is fire-immune.
	- That is a real tactical flip. Play it.
- **Gary arrives:** he must hold a sector at least 100 ft from Jörmun.
	- Sania handles him. He follows the plan well and does not improvise.
	- The sight of him invalidates point four of Thay's orders, which is R-6 for Thay.
- **The Cult enters as a third side:** it goes for the bank, against both armies.
	- If Thay breaks first, the Cult is what is left at the bank.
- **Thay routs:** pursuit. Past the region's edge it needs Jörmun's order (Legate Doctrine).
	- Out of contact, Cuneus rolls 3d6 vs 12.
	- Vraxis's remaining lancers cover the retreat.

**Discoveries live here:** C-1, C-2, S-1, S-2, S-4, S-5, S-6, S-7, S-8, S-10, S-11, S-12, S-14; prisoners P1–P6 and P9–P12. **The reverse captures (R-1 to R-7) also live here.**

**Trackers changed:** Mass Combat results by round · casualties on both sides · bank charge (or dump) · Threshold souls trapped · layers revealed (cap 3 a side) · Enemy Knowledge on both sides · custody holdings · named deaths and captures.

**Partial success:**
- The Crown holds but the bank is dumped or breached.
- Or the garrison is broken holding it.
- Or the commander escapes with C-2.

**Failure continuation:**
- Thay takes the bank and withdraws with charged cells, or holds the site.
- Jörmun is the Crown's only defender left, and the Legate mandate is under pressure.
- Session 3 becomes the counter-raid or negotiation opening, not an aftermath.

**End state, then transition:** the field at dusk. The ice holds the Thayan dead standing. **Cut** to the morning after.

## 8.3 Session 3 — "Ice and Custody"

- The dead are counted.
- Thayan souls are held in the Threshold's ice.
- The prisoners are under guard.

**The player's objective:** turn victory or loss into leverage. That means proof for Arik, intelligence for the Veil, a settlement with the Bedine, and a decision on pursuit.
**Thay's objective:** recover its commander, or ransom him. Learn what the Crown really fields. Revise its doctrine against Jörmun.

**Encounter sequence:**

2. **Interrogations** (7.9–7.10). One full extraction per prisoner per day. Whoever holds custody runs it.
3. **E10, custody.** Jörmun rules himself, lets Jin and Virelle contest it, or seals everything for Arik (7.6, 7.15). Reaction rolls for the loser.
4. **Politics:**
	- **The spirit-speakers and S-12:** showing Thay's springs survey turns the Bedine.

6. **Advancement close** (06 §6.6):
	- Jörmun's XP and CP.
	- Decisive-battle banking for every named NPC (+2 featured, +1 present).
	- **Veterancy rolls** for surviving units if the battle was won.

**Response branches:**
- **Pursuit authorised:** this opens the Hollow Reach arc's first move, and needs the renegotiation proof.
- **Pursuit refused:** Thay regroups. The surge is 10 days out; the relief column is 30–40 days.
- **The Cult thread** (C-3 held): a follow-on operation targeting the dracolich body, with Jörmun's empty phylactery at its centre.

**Trackers changed:**

- Legate mandate status.
- Bedine standing, by clan.
- Enemy Knowledge Composite, both sides.
- Advancement registers.
- The Deferred Dice row, which closes as Resolved.

**End state:** the module closes. Its consequences hand off to the parley thread, Hollow Reach, the Cult thread and the Susanoo thread.

### Beat 0 — The night before (−8 h to −1 h)
**Scene setting.** The Crown at night in Uktar.
- The desert has dropped below freezing. Inside the territory it is colder again: Jörmun's cold bleeds 10 miles out, and the sand rings like struck tin underfoot.
- Seliara's ice wall forms a 200-ft semicircle around the summit, 15–20 ft high, smooth and grey-blue on the outside. It sweats a faint mist where the aquifer feeds it from below.
- Inside, the crenellated fighting platform is lit by two shuttered lanterns per section. Mago will not allow more.
- The 40-ft kill zone gap is black. Below it the southern approaches fall away in rock steps and wind-cut gullies toward the open erg.
- The eight springs murmur. Their 200-ft precincts are marked with white stones, and nobody fights inside them.
- Smell: cold mineral water, lamp oil, horse, and the sour metal of the charge bank at its 350-ft standoff.
- Sound: wind over the wall's lip, *hhhhsss*, and the *tik… tik* of the ice re-freezing where it sweats.

**Positions at the start of the beat.**
- **Jörmun:** forward of the wall, in or past the kill zone. The aura rule keeps him there.
- **Mago:** on the platform at the gate section.
- **The garrison (~30):** in three watches on the walls.
- **Vorian's screen:** at the gate.
- **Khorzad:** high and dark over the summit.
- **Charge bank:** at its standoff. 04 §2 gives its placement; it sits where the seize team will try to land.

**What Thay does tonight (GM, secret).** Run this off the Mage Tracker.
1. **Scrying.** Every mage with *scrying* tries it once on the Crown or on Jörmun, taking Khorzad's −4. **Each success fills one line of Thay's Enemy Knowledge** (05). Each failure is noticed on 3d6 ≤ Jörmun's or Seliara's relevant sense (the sonde-lightning "eye in the storm" from 7.x is the tell, if Phase A is built).
2. **Planting.** One Herder or Butcher buries a ghoul detachment of up to a third of the pool under the sand of the approach. They will rise behind the first wave.
3. **Fog and mirage.** A Wizard 9+ casts *mirage arcana* to hide a dry wadi used as an approach (F5). Approach scouting (05) now suffers the concealment penalty.
4. **Stone shaping.** A Breaker uses *stone shape* to cut steps into the rock below the stone wall's 120-ft run. The marks are seen at dawn: Search or Vision roll, +2 for anyone who knows masonry (Mago).

**GM triggers.**
- **Khorzad sees fog where there should be none:** a pause point is not yet earned. Tell the player that the southern gully "looks wrong", nothing more.
- **Jörmun goes hunting in the dark:** let him. Every mage he finds now is a retinue that never fights. Thay's answer is the Survivor's *contingency* and the fog. The Hand is not here yet unless F14 fired.
- **A player asks about the ice under fire:** it does not melt. Thay knows this from Marduck's survivors (S062), so expect no fire on the ice tomorrow.

### Beat 1 — Dawn: the dead walk first (Mass Combat round 1)
**Scene setting.**
- Light comes up grey, then hard white off the erg. Long shadows run north from every rock.
- Out on the approach, the sand **moves**. The planted ghouls come up first, shaking grit from their joints. Behind them the mass of zombies and skeletons climbs out of the gullies in lines.
- They march in step. They aren't fast, and they make no sound but *crunch-crunch-crunch*, thousands of feet on frozen sand.
- The living Thayan column is **behind** them, out of the aura's 10-mile reach? **No.** Inside the Threshold, the aura pressure is Jörmun's, not distance's. The column holds 400–600 yards back, and **the mages stand among their own dead**, red robes like wounds in a grey field.

**Why the dead come first (tell the table through action, not exposition).**
- The dead do not fear.
- They soak the aura and breath.
- They find Jörmun. Wherever the dead start freezing solid, that's where he is.
- **Every Herder mage watches where the ice stops their dead,** and that sets Thay's map of the aura.

**Running it.**
- Resolve **Mass Combat round 1** at the table. **Record the live GURPS result** before anyone narrates the outcome.
- Thay's TS = the column (F2) + the sum of Tracker retinues at their quality rungs + revealed layers.
- Strategy: +1 for a cadre of 6+ mages, +2 for 12+.
- Inside the round, run Jörmun's **significant action** as tactical play. 600–800 words of beat if you narrate it; the GM's beats are in the next section.

**Jörmun in the dead (the tactical layer).**
- **Undead cannot be frightened, and their freezing still counts.** The aura pierces immunity (04 §4). The front rank goes stiff, white, and still, and stays standing: a field of frozen statues the living have to climb through.
- **Each breath clears ground and draws the mages' eyes.** After his first breath, every Duellist on the Tracker knows his position. That breaks their scrying uncertainty for free.
- **The mages are 20–60 ft behind their front rank**, inside their own guard. Killing a mage is a choice to go *through* the retinue, and Jörmun may.

**Mago's decisions (d6 if he must act without orders, or play him).**
| d6 | Mago does |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Holds. Nobody shoots until the dead are in the kill zone. "Let the big one have them. We take what climbs." |
| 3–4 | Sends archers to the stone wall's run to watch the *stone shape* steps (Beat 0) |
| 5 | Sends a runner to Vorian: "Gate holds. Don't you go out there." |
| 6 | Sees a red robe in range and orders volleys on it. **This is the right call.** Reward it: if it lands, it's a significant action |

**Voices.**
- **Mago,** low, an orc's gravel and centurion's economy: *"Shields up, heads down. The dead don't aim. The ones in red do."*
- **A Thayan Herder,** half-chanting, half-ordering in Mulhorandi to the dead: *"Forward. Forward. Again."*
- **Vorian,** inside the sealed suit, voice flattened through the plate: *"I can be in the cold for a minute. Maybe two. Tell me when."*

### Beat 2 — The breach (Mass Combat round 2)

2. **Breakers pair up on the ice.**

	- Herders throw every mass body they have into the hole during that window.
	- **If only one Breaker has *disintegrate*,** the hole doesn't open. Thay switches to the stone wall: a *passwall* through the 120-ft run, or *transmute rock to mud* under it.
3. **Butchers clear the parapet.**
	- *Cloudkill* rolls onto the platform: 5 HD or less die outright, 6+ save.
	- Then *circle of death* on any packed section: Fort negates, and it kills creatures under 9 HD.

### Beat 3 — The seize (round 2 or 3)

- The capacitor cells sit in their racks at the 350-ft standoff. If Phase A is built there are five Standard cells, each the size of a cask (04 §2), wrapped in insulated crystal lattice.
- The air there tastes of copper. Hair lifts on arms.
- Nobody stands within 30 ft of it without reason. Its dump radius is common knowledge in the garrison.

**What happens.**
- A **Seizer** (Wizard 9+, *teleport* or *dimension door*) arrives *on* the bank with their guard: up to a third of their pool in ghasts or ghouls (by level), plus any wight officer.
- **The order is take it whole** (C-2a), so they set up a perimeter. **They do not damage it.**
- If two Seizers are on the Tracker, the second arrives the next round with *wall of force* to seal the approach.

**Empire triggers.**
- **The bank is held by Thay:** E11 is now live. Jörmun or Mago can order the dump (1d6 per stored charge, 30-ft radius per 25 charges). **The 350-ft standoff keeps it off the springs.** It kills the Seizer, the guard and the prize. Thay loses the reason it came.
- **The bank is held and *not* dumped:** Thay's objective is half done. Every round it stays held, Thay is +1 on the next recompute for morale (R; the Holding Card supersedes this when it's written).
- **Anyone goes for the Seizer:** the Seizer has a contingency (if CL 11+). It fires at low hp, and the guard holds on without them, mindless, until cut down.

**Voices.**
- **The Seizer,** to their guard: *"Ring it. Nothing touches it. Nothing."*
- **Mago,** if the dump is his call: *"How far does it go? …Fine. Clear the north side. Then I do it."*

### Beat 4 — The answer to the cold (whenever Jörmun commits)
Run 7.12 (Hearthward / Cold-Breakers / Akhet Brazier) if F7 rolled it. If not, **every Duellist on the Tracker runs a partial Cold-Breakers sequence:**
1. ***Slow*, *fear*, *enervation*:** cheap saves to burn his Legendary Resistance (3/day). **Count them aloud at the table.** The player should feel the three going.
2. ***Dimensional anchor*** once the resistances are spent. Now he can't leave by magic.
3. ***Forcecage*** if a Wizard 13 is on the Tracker and has the 7th left.
4. ***Waves of exhaustion*** to grind.
5. **The dead screen:** Herders push every mass body between Jörmun and the casters. Each frozen body is one more thing to break before he reaches a mage.

**What Jörmun has against it.** He moves first: the aura means the ground near him is his. He kills the Duellist before the third save if he can find them, and breath doesn't need a target line through the dead. Gary, if present: cold-vulnerable, but fire-immune and stands under the Brazier. The Hand's priests (Nym, Lorne) and Teodric's anchor if the Hand is fighting for him.

**Jörmun's XP note (6.6).** Personal kills count per creature. Mages killed here count in full; mass dead frozen by the aura count only if the GM rules the aura is "overcome personally" (it is his). That stays the 6.6 ruling.

### Beat 5 — Raising inside the Threshold (every round, E9)
- Every Herder with a free pool tries *animate dead* on fresh corpses: Thay's own first (C-2a point six), then the garrison's.
- **The Frozen Threshold contest** applies (mechanics in Phase 9; until then the GM decides by 3d6 vs the mage's level + 4 and records it as R).
- **Success:** the new dead join that mage's retinue at the retinue's quality.
- **Failure:** the corpse stays a corpse, and its soul is held in the ice. Increment **Threshold souls trapped**.
- **When a Thayan mage watches their own dead refuse to rise,** say it in their voice: *"…It isn't answering. The body isn't — there's nothing in it."* That line is a clue for the whole Thayan command (R-6-adjacent) and for the players.

### Beat 7 — The break and withdrawal
**When Thay breaks** (the live result says so, or the commander falls or flees):
- **Contingencies fire.** Every CL 11+ mage still on the Tracker vanishes at low hp or on the commander's signal. **Capture needs *dimensional anchor*, the Hand, or speed.** Tell the player plainly after the first one goes: *"He was there, and then he wasn't. Not invisible. Gone."*
- **The rearguard.** Mindless dead left behind with their last order: "hold". They keep fighting. Clearing them is the aftermath of Session 3.
- **The baggage burner.** One mage fires the commander's baggage if capture looks likely (C-2). A Spot or Vision roll sees the red robe walk *toward* the wagons, not away. That's the tell.
- **Pursuit:** per §8.2, with Cuneus at 3d6 vs 12 out of contact. Beyond the region's edge it needs Jörmun's order.

**End-of-session tracker check.** Mage rows: alive, captured, fled by contingency, dead. Retinues: active, idle (master dead), destroyed. Bank: intact, held, dumped. Threshold souls trapped. Garrison count. Live result log, one line per pause.

**Closing image.**
- The field at dusk. The ice holds the Thayan dead standing where the aura caught them, hundreds of them, white and still in their ranks.
- Here and there, a body whose master died mid-command still stands **without** ice, waiting for an order that will never come. When the wind gusts, they sway.
- **Cut** to the morning after.
