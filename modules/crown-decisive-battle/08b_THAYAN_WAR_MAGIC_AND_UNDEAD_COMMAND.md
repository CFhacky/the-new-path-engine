> **What this replaces.** F13 (a single undead contingent rolled by size) is **superseded**. Thay's undead are no longer a block. They are **many retinues, one per mage**, and each mage both casts and commands. The +3 undead-quality weighting still applies to every retinue.

## 1. Force model: mages are the army's skeleton
- **Every Thayan mage commands undead (Chad).** Each mage's **control pool = 4 HD of undead per caster level.** This is the SRD *animate dead* control limit, applied to every Thayan mage of any school by Thayan training and binding (R, per Chad's ruling).
- **How many mages ride with the column** (new **F19**, sealed at fire): one mage per N soldiers.

	| 3d6 | Ratio | Basis |
	|---|---|---|
	| 3–8 | 1 per 300 | Vraxhal's garrison norm (14 per 4,200) |
	| 9–12 | 1 per 200 | — |
	| 13–15 | 1 per 120 | — |
	| 16–18 | 1 per 75 | Akhet-Senet's norm (~200 per ~15k) |

	There is a minimum of 3 mages; the commander (F17) is extra. Examples: a 1,700-man column at 1 per 200 brings 8 mages; at 1 per 75 it brings 22.
- **Each mage's level** (new **F20**, rolled per mage): 3–8 Wizard 7 · 9–12 Wizard 9 · 13–15 Wizard 11 · 16–18 Wizard 13.
- **Each mage's retinue.** Pool = 4 × caster level, in HD. **What fills it** follows the mage's level (SRD *create undead* and *create greater undead* caster-level bands):
	- **Any level:** skeletons and zombies (2 HD human warrior zombies, 1 HD skeletons). This is the mass.
	- **CL 11 and below:** ghouls. **CL 12–14:** ghasts (by *create undead*).
	- **CL 15 and up:** shadows; at CL 16–17, wraiths (by *create greater undead*). Only the commander or Vraxis reaches these.
	- **Default mix:** half the pool in mass dead, a third in the level's best *create undead* type, and the rest in a personal guard.
	- **Quality:** each retinue rolls 3d6+3 (05 §9a).
- **Worked example.** A Wizard 9 controls 36 HD: 9 zombies (18 HD), 6 ghouls (12 HD), and a guard of 3 skeletons plus 1 ghast-equivalent. That is one Mass Combat element only once retinues are grouped by fives. A Wizard 13 controls 52 HD.
- **Households (F7 3–10)** are a mage with retinue by definition, sized by the same pool rule. The F8 household sizes stand as the living retinue on top.
- **The Delegation (C5)** is 12 Red Wizards. Under this ruling they are 12 retinues too, and their bodyguard of 40 is the living part. Scaffold note: Zaltor's own level and specialty stay unrevealed.

**Mass Combat translation.**
- Retinues group into undead elements of about 500 HD-weighted bodies. Quality comes from the 3d6+3 roll; TS follows Battle Layer §4.
- **The mage cadre is a force multiplier, not TS:** +1 Strategy with any cadre of 6 or more; +2 with 12 or more (Battle Layer §4, cap +2).
- **Killing mages breaks retinues.** When a mage dies or flees, their mindless dead stand idle or wander. That is a **significant action** with a direct Mass Combat consequence: remove that retinue's TS at the next pause point.

## 2. How Thayan mages fight: the repertoire by phase
SRD spell levels are given in brackets. The GM picks from these lists by the mage's level, never beyond it.

### Phase A — Before contact (Days 1–3)
- **Watching:** *scrying* [4] and *arcane eye* [4]; *greater scrying* [7] for Vraxis-tier casters. Targets: the bank, the wedge, Jörmun's position. Khorzad's −4 against scrying at the Crown applies.
- **Hiding the approach:** *fog cloud* [2] by day; *mirage arcana* [5] to dress a dry wadi as open sand; *invisibility sphere* [3] around scouts.
- **Shaping the field:** *stone shape* [4] cuts firing steps into the approach rock. Ghouls are planted under sand in the kill zone (their own ambush).

### Phase B — Opening the Crown (contact, rounds 1–3)
- **The stone wall:**
	- *passwall* [5] opens a gate-sized hole through the 120-ft stone wall.
	- *transmute rock to mud* [5] undermines a wall section's footing.
	- *stone shape* [4] seals the defenders' sally ports.
- **The ice wall** (S062: aquifer-fed, self-regenerating, does not melt under fire):
	- *disintegrate* [6] removes a 10-ft cube of it.
	- **R (design, Gate D):** a breach refills in **1d4 rounds**. Thay must push dead through the gap before it closes. That is the job of the mass retinues.
	- Fire doesn't work on it, and every Thayan mage knows that from Marduck's survivors at the Siege.
- **Screening the assault:** *solid fog* [4] over the kill zone stops archers and the Moranth sharpers. *Wall of force* [5] blocks the gate from inside after a breach.
- **Clearing the walls:**
	- *cloudkill* [5] rolls onto the parapet. The ~22 Legion men there are its targets.
	- *fireball* [3] and *lightning bolt* [3] in massed volleys.
	- **Circle of death** [6] (Fort negates; it kills creatures under 9 HD) is aimed at the garrison. **This is how a few mages can empty a wall,** and it is why the garrison should be told to hold behind cover.

### Phase C — The bank grab (rounds 2–6)
- **Seize team.** *Dimension door* [4] or *teleport* [5] puts a mage with a guard retinue (ghasts, a wight officer) **on the bank itself**, using *scrying* to see the destination first.
- Their order is "take it whole" (C-2a), so they **guard the bank against a dump**. They don't loot it.
- The empire's answer is the bank-dump decision (E11), or getting there first.

### Phase D — Answering Jörmun (any round he commits)
- **Cold-Breakers doctrine (7.12b)** if that layer is present; partial versions from any mages otherwise:
	1. *resist energy (cold)* [2] on the line.
	2. Cheap save-or-suffer spells to strip his Legendary Resistance (3/day): *slow* [3], *fear* [4], *enervation* [4].
	3. *Dimensional anchor* [4] to pin him.
	4. *Forcecage* [7] (Vraxis-tier or cadre).
	5. *Waves of fatigue* [5] and *waves of exhaustion* [7] to grind him.
- **The undead screen:** mass dead are thrown into his aura and breath to soak them. The aura pierces immunity, so they freeze, but each one is a body between Jörmun and a mage.
- **Against Gary**, if he appears: **cone of cold** [5]. Gary is cold-vulnerable, and every mage who sees a red dragon reaches for it first. Thay's anti-aerial habits, built against Jörmun, do double duty.
- **Against Khorzad:** *lightning bolt* volleys and *dimensional anchor* if he lands.

### Phase E — Raising as they go (every round)
- *Animate dead* [4] on fresh corpses, filling each mage's free pool HD. *Create undead* [6] on officers' bodies.
- **The Frozen Threshold contest (E9).** Inside the 10-mile territory the dead's souls are held in Jörmun's ice. Raising runs as a contest every round, with mechanics in Phase 9. Thay's orders already say to test it on their own fallen first (C-2a, point six).
- *Control undead* [7] (Vraxis-tier) can take over a rival's or an enemy's raised dead. That includes Lorne's Undead Servitor.

### Phase F — Withdrawal
- **Contingency** [6]: Red Wizards of 11th level and up carry a contingent *teleport* or *dimension door* that fires at low hp. This is doctrine (R). It is why capture needs *dimensional anchor*, the Hand, or sheer speed.
- **Rearguard:** retinues are left behind to hold while the mages leave. Mindless dead fight on with no orders. Clearing them afterwards is its own aftermath scene.
- **Burning the baggage** (C-2): one mage is tasked to *fireball* the commander's baggage if capture looks likely.
