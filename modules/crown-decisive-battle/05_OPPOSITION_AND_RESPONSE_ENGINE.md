## 1. The two-sided layer model
Each side's order of battle has three tiers:
- **Visible:** seen by the other side without a roll.
- **Hidden:** committed but concealed. Scouting rolls against its concealment. **Cap: 3 per side.**
- **Reserve:** exists, must be called, arrives on a clock, costs something to commit. Reserves outside the battle window are listed but do not arrive.

Layer card columns: Asset | Side | Tier | Strength (Mass Combat: Quality, TS) | Concealment | Arrival | Authorised by | Exposure cost | Source.

TS per ~500-troop element (Battle Layer §4, [DIAL]): Regular 40, Veteran 60, Elite 90. Ranged/artillery ×1.2; air ×2. Mage cadres add +1/+2 to the Strategy roll, not TS. Fortification enters only through the holding card (unwritten, C14).

## 4. Scouting
- For each hidden layer, the other side makes **one** roll with its best available scout: **3d6 vs scout skill + concealment.**
	- **Thay:** Red Wizard scrying, 20 (Annex, monthly detection skill; I for tactical use). −4 against targets at the Crown (Khorzad's Contested Sky).
	- **Empire:** best of Khorzad, Ersk, Cuneus (Per 12), the Vigil's observers, Gary (Per 14) if present. Skill values for Khorzad, Ersk and the Vigil come from their sheets in Phase 9 (SR).
- **Results:**
	- Success: the layer is known to exist.
	- Margin 5+: its size too.
	- Critical success: existence, size and arrival.
	- Failure: unseen.
	- Critical failure: false intelligence. The scouting side believes in a phantom layer, or a count one band too low (sealed coin: F12).
- Each side plans against its own picture.

## 5. Reveals and pause points
- An unrevealed layer enters at its F9/F10/F11 arrival. **Every arrival is a pause point** (C13 extended to reinforcements, either side).
- At each pause point Mass Combat is recomputed from that moment with the new TS, and the **live GURPS result is recorded**. No formula is supplied.
- Exposed layers update the other side's knowledge (Enemy Knowledge Composite) after the battle.
