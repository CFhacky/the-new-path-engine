## 1. The Veil's view

**Modifiers:**

**Results:**
- **Crit success:** learns the site's vulnerability: bank-as-bomb, or Node Two's water dependency.

- **Success:** learns that a charge site exists.
- **Marginal:** a rumour.
- **Failure:** nothing.
- **Crit failure:** misreads it as a ritual site or a weapon test. That misreading is a hook of its own.

## 3. Bank-as-bomb
- **Catastrophic dump:** **1d6 per stored charge** (R20), Reflex DC 22 half, across a radius of **30 ft per 25 charges** (R21).
  - A full Gate Zero bank (125 charges) does 125d6 across 150 ft.
  - A full Stormring Pillar (120) does 120d6 across 144 ft.
  - The Capacitor spec calls a charged Pillar "a demolition event", and these numbers are that.
- **Triggers:**
  - deliberate dump
  - overload: more than 5 charges at once on a cell, Fort-equivalent DC 18 or it cracks
  - physical breach of a housing, Breakable DR 6
- **Chekhov:** a cell absorbs lightning within ~60 ft. A hostile caster who throws lightning at a full bank is feeding it. Whether forced intake into a full cell counts as overload is open (Charge Accounting Rules, Open Rulings). An enemy with a crit-success detection result will find that question.
- **Siting rules:** the Crown addendum sets a 350-ft standoff from the springs. At Node Two the compound sits inside the radius (see the Node Two page).

## 4. E-07 — the crystalline horror
- **Source:** Desert Corridor Survey, hook 1.1 "The Singing Sands". E-07 is a ~200-ft wind-eroded amphitheatre with grown crystal veins. They resonate at **echo-crystal frequencies** and repeat a Netherese distress beacon about 4,000 years old.
- **Beneath it:**
  - a survival vault with three arcanists in stasis
  - a resonance amplifier
  - a sealed chamber holding a **crystalline horror** (CR 12 construct). It "consumes crystalline structures to grow… dormant because there was nothing left to eat. Activating the vault's systems will wake it."
  - A Shade Enclave survey team is two days behind any party.
- **Placement (R18):** **40 miles northeast of the Crown.** Phylornel is 21 miles northeast, so E-07 lies about 19 miles beyond it on roughly the same bearing. That separation is derived and assumes the same bearing exactly.
- **Chekhov:**
  - The hook says extended exposure makes **network-linked** crystals broadcast the distress pattern. The Array is bus-linked, not network-linked, until Phase E. Whether a bus counts is an open ruling.
  - Once Phase D is built, the Array is the largest concentration of echo crystal in eastern Anauroch. If anyone wakes the horror, the Crown is the obvious meal.
- **Stats:** none written in either system. Stat it before it can appear (Wave 5 or a module).

## 5. Phylornel

- It sits just outside the 20-mile range for the ±2 Netherese modifier. A node or survey camp placed northeast of the Crown can bring it into range.
