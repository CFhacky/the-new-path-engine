## Position & why here
- **37 miles south of the Crown, on the line of the single principal southbound arterial** that BR-1 plans (Crown → one defended southbound arterial → Black Road). That arterial is **not authorized**. Node Two stands on its projected alignment.
- **Purpose:** a recharge point a full day's off-road drive from the Crown. A Wardrake on flat cores makes 50–70 cross-country miles a day (Doctrine Note 7), so Node Two lets the wedge range south of the Crown and come back with charge.
- **Not a network waystation.** Arterial waystation bays run at about 25-mile intervals. Node Two at 37 miles falls between the first and second of those, and it is not coupled to the Crystal Network.

## Physical description
- **Terrain:** open erg edge, among the dunes. **No water on site; all water is hauled from the Crown** (R13).
- **Compound:** 80 × 100 ft, palisaded (R22). It holds:
  - the bank vault: a Gate Zero bank of 5 Standard cells, footlocker-sized
  - one vehicle bay with a sealed receptacle block
  - the bus module and the sonde winches
  - a covered post and cistern tank for eight
- **Bank standoff (derived):** a full 125-charge bank dumps across 150 ft, which is more than the compound measures. The vault is therefore dug in and bermed at the compound's downwind end. The post sits upwind, and any breach of the bank affects the whole compound. Treat the guard post as inside the blast.
- **Sondes** fly from anchors outside the palisade on 400-ft tethers.
- **Sensory baseline for scenes:** wind-scoured sand hissing against the palisade, ozone after a lightning night, and hot dust and grit in every seam. The nearest water is 37 miles north.

## Garrison
- **8 standing, rotated from Gaius Cuneus's lent Legion section** (R14, R15). While Node Two is manned, the wedge fields 12 of its 20.
- The post answers to Cuneus in the field. The Crown, and Centurion Mago's ~30, do not supply it.
- Seliara keys the node herself (LOG-817), and the month-miss rule applies.

## Supply
- Water: a Roadrider run from the Crown, a 74-mile round trip, about weekly. That costs ~2.5 charges per run, about 10 a month.
- Food and parts come on the same run.

## Threats
- **Vaelthrix's phylactery** lies about 40 miles south (Tinderbox Atlas, Cluster 4). The reference point is not stated. If it is measured from the Crown, Node Two sits close to it. See the Hazard and Threat Annex.
- The site is open, waterless and has no Khorzad overhead, so no −4 against aerial observation. Detection rolls are in the Hazard and Threat Annex.
