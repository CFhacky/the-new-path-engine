### Sacred-precinct exclusion
- The eight springs are sanctified ground in Bedine cosmology (Legate Region). Spirit-speaker blessing on the water grid is essential, and Naima bint Hasan watches Jörmun carefully.
- **Precinct:** a **200-ft buffer around each spring mouth** (R12). No Array element is allowed inside it: no node, bank, bay, sonde anchor, winch or Stormring. No sonde tether may run over a spring mouth, because a strike-arrested sonde drops half its tether (200 ft) before the brake catches it.
- **Bank standoff (derived):** a full Gate Zero bank (125 charges) dumps across a **150-ft radius** (R21: 30 ft per 25 charges). The bank therefore sits at least **350 ft from any spring mouth** (the precinct plus the dump radius), and at least 150 ft from the bays, barracks and cistern. A Stormring Pillar at full charge (120) needs the same 144-ft clearance.
- The Al-Rashid share is "3 springs to Bedine, 5 to Crown" (Farouk ibn Rashid's page, Session 12). The precinct covers all eight mouths, whoever's share they are.

### Phase gate and Investment
- **Current state:** Phase 1 (Folly / Shrine); Security 6, Diplomacy 7, Investment 1. Phase 1→2 needs 5 / 3 / 4.
