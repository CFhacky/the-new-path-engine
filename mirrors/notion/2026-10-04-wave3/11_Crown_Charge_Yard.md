## What it adapts from Charge Island
Loudwater's Charge Island holds "echo-core exchange halls, battery works, capacitor vaults, Impression Racks, vehicle and armor charging, load dispatch and RL-overcharge absorption". Powder Island is "separated from capacitor storage". The page gives no layout or dimensions for it.

| Charge Island function | Charge Yard | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Echo-core exchange hall | **Kept.** Swap-out counter for racked spare cores | The battery bank becomes a building |
| Battery works | **Kept, small.** Racks and a core test bench | Cores go back to the Institut for service (BOM) |
| Capacitor vaults | **Kept.** The Crown bank moves here from its Phase A pit | Phase D tiers fit here |
| Impression Racks | **Not built** | They convert generator output into charge. The Crown has no generator; storm intake replaces them |
| Vehicle charging | **Kept.** Three bays, one for each lent vehicle | Lent section: two Wardrakes, one Roadrider |
| Armor charging | **Excluded** | The Soul Ember Core conflict is unreconciled |
| Load dispatch | **Kept.** The bus office for the Crown node and the Node Two line | Section 3 of the Accounting Rules |
| RL-overcharge absorption | **Reserved** | Phase E only, on coupling |
| Powder separation | **Kept.** No munitions inside the bank's dump radius | Charge Island principle |

## Physical description
- **Footprint:** 150 × 200 ft (R17). The yard sits on the Crown's southern approaches, outside the 200-ft spring precinct.
- **Layout (derived from the dump radius):**
  - The vault takes the yard's far end, dug in and bermed.
  - A full Gate Zero bank dumps across 150 ft, and each added 25 charges adds 30 ft. The vault therefore sits at the end farthest from the barracks and the cistern. At Phase D bank sizes the radius runs past the yard wall, so the Phase D vault must be sited against the 350-ft rule on the Crown addendum.
  - Three bays and the exchange hall stand along the near wall. The dispatch office sits between them.
- **Sensory baseline for scenes:** ozone and hot crystal, the tick of cooling cores in the racks, sand on the bay floors, and the bus humming under load. Under storm the louvres rattle.

## Cost and build
- **Civil works:** 18,000 gp (R16, within the Frontier Phase 2 band of 12,000–25,000).
- **Fit-out:**
  - bays at the open receptacle price
  - spare cores at 2,000 gp each
  - added cells at Capacitor prices
- **Build time:** 1 week per 10,000 gp, so the civil works take about 1.8 weeks. With Formicorps it is ×3, about 4 days.
- **Complications:** a d20 at each phase boundary (Deferred Dice, construction complications row).
- **Staff:** 2 specialists at 30 gp each a month. They are generated only when the yard is built, and each takes a loyalty check vs 10–13.

## Garrison
The Charge Yard falls inside the Crown's ~30 under Centurion Mago (D9). It adds no separate post.
