## The road shape the Array must fit (sourced)
- **Topology (BR-1):** "ranch feeders → Crown of Eight Springs → one principal defended southbound arterial → Black Road → east/west. No high-capacity northern bypass… No route through Crown's sacred precinct."
- **Hierarchy (SH-1):**
  - a continental trunk south of the Crown
  - **one principal heavy road from the Crown to the hinge**
  - feeders ending at the Crown
  - local tracks
- **Laterals (SH-1):** feeders must not be linked into a high-capacity lateral trunk. Emergency detours "must not connect a trunk directly to ranch districts". The graph test rejects any lateral by design.
- **Distance:** Southern Hinge H1 lies about 408–422 straight-line miles from the Crown. This is a planning trace, and gate SG01 is open.
- **Standards:** the arterial is 24 ft wide, and the Black Road is 48 ft. Waystation bays sit at about 25-mile intervals. Crystal nodes run at a standard 2-mile spacing.
- **Industrial exclusion (SH-1):** the sacred and civic-water precincts are closed to industry, and industrial water entitlement is zero.

## Where the Array sits on it (adjudicated from LOG-817 and Wave 2)
- **Node Two lies 37 miles out on the principal southbound alignment**, which is the line BR-1 and SH-1 both call for. That line is the only one the Array is allowed to follow, so the Array has no lateral nodes. A third node later goes farther down the same line.
- **The node line** (Phase B) is 19 node modules at 2-mile spacing, laid along that alignment. It is a private charge line, not the arterial and not the Crystal Network. If the arterial is later built, the line becomes its node run, and coupling then follows Phase E rules.
- **Waystation fit:** arterial waystations would fall at about 25 and 50 miles. Node Two at 37 miles falls between them. If the arterial comes, either Node Two moves (its bank and bay relocate to the 25- or 50-mile waystation) or it stays as an off-standard depot. That is an open ruling.
- **Crown connector:** the Crown end of the arterial runs off the southern approaches, outside the 200-ft precinct around each spring mouth. The Charge Yard sits at the connector's head, so vehicles charge before they take the road.
- **No arterial yet:** the wedge works Crown ↔ Node Two off-road. That is under a day for a Wardrake (50–70 cross-country miles a day on flat cores) and half a day for a Roadrider (100–140). Each hard cross-country day adds maintenance debt at 1 workshop day per 2 force-march days (Doctrine Note 7).
