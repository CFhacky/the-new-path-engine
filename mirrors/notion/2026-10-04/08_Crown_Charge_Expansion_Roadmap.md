## Gates in detail
**Crystal supply.** Cultivated echo crystal and artificer hours are the bottleneck for every crystal part (BOM). Gold is not the limit on a bank. Enchantment time at CL 13+, fractal-cut specialist labor and crystal supply are (Capacitor spec).

**Seliara.**
- She keys both nodes herself (bound, 4 Oct 2026), and the glacier survey slips a season as a result.
- While nodes are built or tended she rolls 3d6 vs 12 each month (Deferred Dice).
- A miss brings a certified Athenaeum operator the following month. Joint Crystal Networks work then takes −2, dropping by 1 for each full month they work together without a miss.
- Serapha Moonfire then knows the site exists.
- Her presence on site also halves the time to the Crown's Investment threshold for Phase 1 → 2 (Crown row).

**Investment.**
- Each finished node adds +1 to the Crown's Investment track (bound).
- The Crown's own monthly Investment roll stays 3d6 vs 11 (D9), with modifiers capped at +6 (Frontier Framework).
- Investment stands at 1. Phases A and B together bring it to 3, so one more successful Investment roll reaches the 4 needed for Phase 2 and opens Phase C.

**Glacier (future gate only).**
- The first glacier is not raised in play. The Glacier Terminus Complex page is PROJECTED.
- Once a glacier exists, the Crown becomes a waystation and its springs supplement the melt (Crown row). Waystation traffic is the demand case for Phase D growth.
- Nothing in Phases A–C depends on the glacier.

**Coupling risk (Phase E).**
- Any rise in the network's coupling multiplier while network RL is 80 or more forces an Instability roll. The tracker sits at RL 100.
- A Netherese site within 20 miles of an active node gives ±2 on the network roll, GM's choice of corrupting or feeding. Phylornel is 21 miles from the Crown, just outside.
- Any node sited nearer to Phylornel brings that modifier in.

**Security.** Every phase grows the stored charge, and with it the bank-as-bomb hazard. The Hazard and Threat Annex (Wave 3, Doc 14) carries the threat side: E-07, Phylornel, the Cult, the Shade Remnant, Thay and the storm's unknown makers.

## Construction
- Each physical build uses the construction sub-routine: 1 week per 10,000 gp, Formicorps ×3 with no cost cut, a d20 complication at each phase boundary or quarterly (held in Deferred Dice), and caster discounts.
- Crystal items (cells, sondes, the Pillar cell) run on their own crafting tracks. They follow hybrid-magic-research for item creation and hybrid-research-invention for the Stormsonde R&D project (Complex, 10 successes, monthly, margin cap 16).
