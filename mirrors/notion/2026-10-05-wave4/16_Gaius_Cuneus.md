## I. LORE & ROLEPLAYING GUIDE

### First sight
Gaius Cuneus stands six foot one in his boots, broad through the chest the way orcs run broad, with the slight forward lean of a man who has spent years under a vehicle ring hatch. His skin is a dark grey-green, sun-cured to leather across the cheekbones, and a white seam of scar runs from his left tusk to the hinge of his jaw where a pike once found the gap between helm and gorget. He keeps his black hair cropped to a quarter inch. His lower tusks are filed blunt, a Legion habit he never broke. His eyes are amber and never settle; they go to the horizon, the map, the horizon again.

He wears Legion Standard power armor with the eagle and axe ground off the breastplate and Jörmun's frost-white sash knotted at the left hip in their place, so the armor says ex-Legion before he opens his mouth. Under it is a sand-coloured gambeson gone salt-white at the armpits. The left vambrace has a map case riveted to it, scratched bright from use, with a grease pencil wired to the lid. On a march he reeks of ozone and hot crystal from the Wardrake's cores and of the clove oil he chews to keep the dust out of his throat. He walks the column at the halt in a fast rolling stride, touching each vehicle's flank as he passes, counting.

### Concept
- An ex-Legion Optio who served out his term and took Jörmun's coin the week he was discharged.
- He commands the mobile wedge: one lent Legion section, two Wardrakes (one a Command variant) and one Roadrider.
- Incisive and daring (LOG-817). He reads a fight two moves ahead and likes to be first to the place it will happen.

### Chain of command
- **Jörmun** holds tactical authority (LOG-817). Cuneus is Jörmun's man, on Jörmun's pay, outside the Legion's chain.
- **The lent section** is Legion personnel under Korgan's standing orders. Its senior sergeant answers to Korgan through the Legion. Cuneus directs the section in the field on Jörmun's authority. Korgan's doctrine works by coordination, not subordination, and Korgan can recall the loan.
- **Centurion Mago** holds the Crown. Cuneus does not command the garrison, and Mago does not command the wedge.
- **Centurion Hektor Vorian** runs the infantry screen under Jörmun. Vorian outranks the Optio Cuneus used to be, and both of them remember it.

### Personality and voice
- Fast, dry, and specific. He talks in distances, times and charge.
- Interrupts with the answer.
- Respects Legion sergeants and resents being asked for his old rank.
- Under pressure his voice gets quieter. When he is wrong, he says so once and moves on.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Thirty miles out, thirty back, and we're on the stowed core. Call it forty-two with the wind."
  - "They're running. That's the moment they decide where we stop. I'd rather decide."
  - "Sergeant, you answer to Korgan. Today Korgan wants that ridge. So do I."

### Flaw: the off-road core clock
Off the arterial there is no trickle-coupling, and the core clock rules (Light Vehicle Program, Doctrine Note 7). Cuneus pushes the clock. He plans to the stowed core instead of the safe turn-back point.

### Temptation: pursuit past the mandate
He has **Overconfidence (12)**. When a beaten enemy runs toward the region's edge, he wants to finish it. The Legate Doctrine counts engagement with Thay's southern frontier beyond a defensive posture as a matter for renegotiation. The pursuit rule in the Wedge and Anvil Doctrine is the check on him.
