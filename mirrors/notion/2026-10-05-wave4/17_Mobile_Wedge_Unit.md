- **Adjudicated arrangement.** A Legion section is normally two 10-man squads. The lent section is reorganised into vehicle teams because the seats force it. The senior squad sergeant keeps Legion command of the section's people and rides with Cuneus.
- **Node Two (Wave 3).** When Node Two is manned, the assault team's 8 dismounts garrison it. W2 stays with the wedge, crewed by its driver and gunner, as a gun carrier with an empty compartment. That compartment shuttles the Node Two rotation, casualties and water. The wedge then fields W1, W2 (crew only) and R1: **12 Legion plus Cuneus**, matching the Wave 3 figure.
- **Maintenance.** The ratio is 1 mechanic to 2 Wardrakes and 1 to 4 Roadriders, which is 1.25 mechanics for this wedge. It has 1, so it runs under ratio. The movement table counts the shortfall as maintenance debt.
