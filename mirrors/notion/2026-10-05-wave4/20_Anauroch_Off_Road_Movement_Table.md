## Modifiers
- **Active sandstorm:** pace ×0.5 (C17), on top of terrain. The core clock does not slow, so reach and radius halve.
- **Charge:** a stopped vehicle draws little. A moving one spends its clock by the hour, not by the mile. Bad terrain therefore costs range as well as time.
- **Maintenance debt:** any day with 6 hours or more off-road is a hard day. Debt is ~1 workshop day per 2 hard days (Note 7), ×1.25 for this wedge's mechanic shortfall.
