> **Status.** This amendment is append-only, and nothing above it is altered. It **clarifies** the mandate and does not modify it. Under §VI a modification takes effect only on mutual agreement and is documented on the Legate's region page. This text becomes binding when Arik and Jörmun agree to it in play. Until then it is PROJECTED guidance.

1. **Mandate alignment.** A mobile reaction force that protects the canal corridor and the eastern frontier serves the mandate envelope: "expansion that makes canal protection more achievable". Doctrine Note 6 of the Light Vehicle Program states the principle. One quick-reaction force at the centre of mass replaces fixed garrisons across the footprint.
2. **Lent, not accumulated.** The Wedge is a Legion-owned loan from Korgan: one 20-person section, two Wardrakes (one Command variant) and one Roadrider (LOG-817). It does not count as "accumulating forces beyond mandate need". Growth past the loan is Jörmun's capital and must be justified as corridor need.
3. **Command.**
   - Jörmun holds tactical authority.
   - Gaius Cuneus commands in the field as Jörmun's hire.
   - The section's Legion command stays with its senior squad sergeant under Korgan's standing orders.
   - Korgan coordinates and does not subordinate, and he may recall the loan.
   - The Summoning Circle compels presence, not obedience.
4. **Limits.** The Wedge does not:
   - engage Thay's southern frontier beyond a defensive posture
   - pursue past the region's edge without Jörmun's order (Wedge and Anvil Doctrine, pursuit rule)
   - recruit from empire-protected populations without empire coordination
   - fight anyone's war on behalf of the empire
5. **Record.** On agreement in play, document this amendment on the Eastern Anauroch — Frostborn Legate Region page per §VI, and pair it with a Canon Change Log entry.
