> **Canon status — PROJECTED.** This is doctrine for a force that has not taken the field in play. Battle math follows the GURPS Mass Combat Battle Layer. Its force-ratio and Combat Results tables are untranscribed, so every battle takes a recorded live GURPS result. Nothing here supplies a formula for them.

## 1. The idea
- **The anvil:** the fixed points.
  - The Crown: ~30 under Centurion Mago, Khorzad overhead.
  - Node Two: 8 when manned.
  - Each node's bank and bay.
- **The wedge:** Gaius Cuneus's mobile force, which strikes what the anvil holds or meets a raid on its approach.
- **Source:** Light Vehicle Program, Doctrine Note 6. One mounted quick-reaction force at the network's centre of mass replaces fixed garrisons, and "stories patrol for free". The region has no arterial yet, so the wedge runs on the core clock (Note 7), and the anvil is where the clock is reset.

## 2. Mandate boundary (Legate Doctrine; Legate Region)
- **The wedge is corridor defence:** protecting canal infrastructure and the eastern frontier. That is inside the mandate.
- **Requires renegotiation:**
  - "engagement with Thay's southern frontier beyond defensive posture"
  - "expansion south into unrelated Anauroch territory"
  - "accumulating forces beyond mandate need"
- **The vehicles are lent, not accumulated.** Growth past the loan is Jörmun's capital and must be justified as mandate need.
- **Korgan** coordinates and does not subordinate. He can recall the loan.

- The wedge moves at the Wardrake's clock.
- A stowed core is half a Wardrake pair, because the two cores run in parallel.
- **Crown to Node Two** is 37 mi, about 6.2 h off-road. A Wardrake arrives with about 38% of its pair. In an active sandstorm the leg takes about 12.3 h, which is more than a pair holds, so it needs the stowed core.
- **Cuneus's flaw:** he plans to the stowed-core radius (45 mi) instead of the pair radius (30 mi). The rule is that **the turn-back point is half charge with no node or stowed core ahead.**

## 4. Supply (hybrid-military-ops, movement and supply only)
- **Daily need:** 3 lb food and 8 lb water per soldier.
  - The wedge (21) needs about 231 lb a day.
  - Node Two (8) needs 88 lb a day, about 616 lb a week.
- **Roadrider cargo** is driver + 1 + 900 lb. One weekly run therefore covers Node Two's food and water. This closes the open cargo item on the Crown's projected books.

## 5. Turtle response
Use it when an active sandstorm closes on the wedge off-road, when it is ambushed, or when a vehicle is immobilised.
1. **Halt and laager.** The Wardrakes go nose-out in a V around the Roadrider. W1 takes the windward point.
2. **Dismount to the lee.** Infantry go to the vehicles' lee, and the medic and mechanic go into the V. This follows the Combined Arms Doctrine: "armored flanks absorb ambush, VIP mobile at center, rear guard prevents pursuit."
3. **Short-halt fire only.** The Maw does not fire on the move (Light Vehicle Program open item).
4. **Hold the clock.** A halted vehicle draws little. Restart when the storm drops or a node is within the turn-back radius.
5. **In the 3.5e engine**, occupants have total cover. The hull is Hardness 10 (12 frontal) with 200 hp.

## 6. Off-road operations
- Move hamada to hamada, and avoid the erg (see the Anauroch Off-Road Movement Table).
- Hard cross-country days build **maintenance debt: about 1 workshop day per 2 force-march days.** The wedge runs one mechanic short of ratio, so count debt at ×1.25.
- Hard stops (Note 7): water above fording depth (~3 ft for a Wardrake), deep marsh, true mountain off any track, dense old growth.

## 7. Pursuit rule
1. **Inside the region and inside the turn-back radius:** Cuneus may pursue.
2. **At the turn-back point:** break off. Jörmun's order can override; Cuneus's judgment cannot.
3. **Across the region's edge, or toward Thay's frontier:** Jörmun's order only. Without it, crossing is mandate drift.
4. **Out of contact with Jörmun while the enemy crosses the line:** Cuneus rolls **3d6 vs 12** (Overconfidence self-control). On a failure he pursues one more leg before breaking off, and the GM sets what that costs. Record every roll.
5. **Contrast:** Wyrmhelm doctrine prohibits pursuit outright. The wedge may pursue, but only inside the line.

## 8. Battle math
- **Skirmishes under 100 troops:** the fused 3.5e/GURPS engine.
- **Battles of 100–5,000:** the GURPS Mass Combat Battle Layer, as run in Iron Dawn.
  - The Battle Layer has no vehicle or mobility feature, and its force-ratio and Combat Results tables are untranscribed.
  - The wedge's effect is therefore **not formulated**. It takes a **recorded live GURPS result** at the table.
  - Fortification enters only through a holding card.
