# The Menagerie — Pairings (authored, 6 Oct 2026)

> **What this is.** How the host's spells and releases work when two of them are on the same field. **Spell pairings are rules as written**: the spells come from the stat blocks and combine by the book. **Release pairings** add one authored effect when both releases are live and overlapping. **Nothing here has happened in play.**
>
> Sources: captains' and lieutenants' stat blocks (rounds 3–4), *The Menagerie — Signature Items* (round 5), *The Menagerie — Releases* (round 6), the rival web (round 2).

## Rules for pairing
- **Spells combine as written.** A spell pairing invents nothing: it is two spells cast in the order shown. The value is in knowing who carries what.
- **Two releases overlapping:** both apply in full. A **named release pairing** adds the one effect listed, and only while both releases are running and their areas overlap.
- **Who gives the order:**
	- A captain orders their own lieutenant.
	- Pairings across divisions take **Lirien** (or the senior captain present, with her approval after the fact).
	- **Rival pairings take Arik.** Nobody else can make those two work together, and neither of them will offer.
- **GURPS:** where a pairing adds an effect, it is given as one line; spell pairings use each spell's own GURPS reading.

# 1. CAPTAIN AND LIEUTENANT (the eight natural pairs)

## I. Quavein + Osmund — *The Storm Office*
- **Spells:** Osmund casts *death ward* on Quavein, then *silence* on the enemy's casters. Quavein walks in under the ward and casts *destruction*, which a *death ward* would have stopped if the enemy had one.
- **Releases (pairing):** Osmund's funeral smoke goes into the gale. The storm carries it through the whole 120 ft. Everything that dies anywhere in the storm rises in the eye beside Quavein, and the zombie cap is 8, not 4.
- **GURPS:** Death Knight Ally Group expanded to the storm's Area 40 yd (max 8).
- **From across the field:** a black-green storm, grey smoke wound through it like a rope, and the dead standing up inside.

## II. Hadda + Kerra — *Second's Rights*
- **Spells:** Kerra's *haste* goes on Hadda before the duel; Hadda's *quickened shield* is already up. If the duel goes wrong, Kerra's *slow* lands on the challenger.
- **Releases (pairing):** Kerra's dueling circle rises inside the lit web. On "ten," the circle's 10d6 force and the web's pull land in the same instant. Every enemy in the circle takes both, and the circle's free attack is Hadda's.
- **GURPS:** Follow-Up: the circle's 10d crushing triggers the web's pull on all bound inside.
- **From across the field:** a ring of light inside a room full of glinting threads, and a woman counting aloud.

## III. Tarvash + Wenna — *The Press-Gang*
- **Spells:** Wenna's *mass suggestion*: "Stay where you are and look up." Tarvash is already in the sky.
- **Releases (pairing):** Wenna's pressed hold their former allies in place. Each pressed creature grapples an enemy as its action, and anything held under a Stoop impact takes the crater **and** both scimitars as criticals.
- **GURPS:** Mind Control Area 13 yd, with pressed Allies imposing Grappled on targets under the Explosion.
- **From across the field:** men holding their friends still, then a hawk's whistle, then a crater.

## IX. Mercy + Faelith — *The Reef Has a Wall*
- **Spells:** none; neither casts. They are the host's silent pair, and Faelith sings when Mercy goes quiet.
- **Releases (pairing):** her line of shields becomes the reef's edge. Nothing swims or walks out of the black water past the wall, and anything that strikes the wall from inside takes its 8d6 rebound and is still in the reef.
- **GURPS:** Force Wall (Line 20 yd) as the boundary of Control Water (Area 33 yd), with Damage Reflection 8d.
- **From across the field:** a sphere of black water with a wall of ghost-shields across one face, and an elf singing badly in front of it.

## X. Zaheda + Patience — *Reading the Margin*
- **Spells:** Zaheda's *wall of thorns* boxes the field, and Patience fights inside it with the flail. He's a tiefling, so cold, fire and electricity resistance 5 lets him stand in her *call lightning storm* longer than anyone else.
- **Releases (pairing):** Patience is learning her cipher. Inside the Page, he can **write a name** too: one ink tiger per round answers to him. His Thrice whirl doesn't harm ink tigers.
- **GURPS:** Ally Group, 1 per turn under the lieutenant's command.
- **From across the field:** a tiefling with a whirling flail, and the ink tigers giving him room.

## XI. Ysmay + Rhun — *The Watch in the Dark*
- **Spells:** none. Rhun carries the flask, stands on her bright-light side, and Osmund (Division I) heals her (see section 2).
- **Releases (pairing):** Rhun plants the pollaxe inside the Night Hunt.
	- Anything moving towards Ysmay is stopped by him where she can see it and it can't see her.
	- His allies-don't-fall clause covers her too, so her deferred damage stays deferred for the full 10 rounds even after his 3 rounds end.
	- Covenant and Revenant together: the pair that won't stay down.
- **GURPS:** Unkillable on the captain extended to the release's full duration.
- **From across the field:** nothing at all. It's dark.

## XII. Ilvaera + Edwyn — *Clean Hands*
- **Spells:** Edwyn's *globe of invulnerability* covers her from spells of 4th level and below. His *dimensional anchor* goes on her target, so it can't blink away from the volley. *Mislead* hides where she really is.
- **Releases (pairing):** Compline's dome stops magic coming in, not going out. Her two-thousand-dart cones fly out of the dome while nothing enters it, and the darts left in the wounded can still carry her spells into the dome's dead zone.
- **GURPS:** Magic Resistance (Area, from outside only) with the user's attacks unaffected.
- **From across the field:** a humming dome, and a storm of steel coming out of it.

## XIII. Dace + Ashavel — *The Challenger Never Arrives*
- **Spells:** Dace's *expansion* and *offensive precognition*. Ashavel has no spells; her Desert Wind maneuvers burn.
- **Releases (pairing):** inside the Sirocco, Dace doesn't need his eyes; precognition sees the next heartbeat, not the sand. Every creature the Sirocco blinds loses its defence against the Strike Before: the save to make his attacks miss doesn't help it.
- **GURPS:** the Strike Before's Rapid Strike ignores Blindness penalties; targets lose Active Defenses while blinded.
- **From across the field:** a wall of burning sand, and a red-lacquered spear in every gap of it.

# 2. SPELL PAIRINGS ACROSS DIVISIONS (rules as written; order Lirien's)

## Osmund + Ysmay — *The Wrong Medicine*
- **The Covenant made useful.** Ysmay's sword makes positive energy hurt her and negative energy heal her.
	- Osmund's *harm* **heals her for 120 HP** (CL 12).
	- Quavein's *heal* would do **150 damage** to her.
- **The rule for any Veil medic:** never heal Ysmay; harm her.
- **From across the field:** a priest lays a hand on a wounded old woman, and she stands up.

## Hadda + Quavein — *The Cage and the Collapse*
- Hadda's barred *forcecage* holds the target; the bars let a spell through.
- Quavein casts *implosion* through the bars, Fort DC 28 or die, again every round he concentrates. The target cannot leave.
- **From across the field:** a cage of light, and something inside it folding.

## Edwyn + Wenna — *The Lieutenants' Table*
- Edwyn's *mind fog*: **−10 on Will saves** in a 20-ft cloud.
- Wenna then casts *dominate person* (DC 24), *feeblemind* or *mass suggestion* into it. Against a fogged target her enchantment DCs are effectively 10 higher.
- **The result:** two lieutenants can take a captain off the board.

## Naevys + Mercy — *The Reef Freezes*
- Mercy's Reef is up (a release). Naevys casts *cone of cold* into the water.
- **Pairing:** the sphere freezes solid. Every creature in it is entombed: helpless, Str DC 26 to break out.
- Mercy leaves first through his Writ's Shadow Jump; he sees it coming.
- **GURPS:** Binding 26 (Engulfing, Area 33 yd), then the Reef ends.
- **From across the field:** a black sphere goes white, and it doesn't make a sound either.

## Zaheda + Tarvash — *The Whirlwind and the Hawk*
- Zaheda's *whirlwind* lifts every Large or smaller creature it catches into the air.
- Tarvash, releasing, stoops onto the column. A target in the air takes the impact, both blades as criticals, **and** the fall.
- **From across the field:** a cyclone, and something falling into it from far above.

## Zaheda + Brunna — *The Thornbush*
- Zaheda's *wall of thorns*, and then Brunna's Larder pins everyone into the thorns.
- A pinned creature takes the wall's thorn damage every round it hangs there: 25 minus its AC, without its Dex bonus.
- **From across the field:** a shrike's larder, literally.

## Kerra + Aerendyl — *Mud*
- Kerra's *transmute rock to mud* makes the stone floor 10 ft of mud.
- Aerendyl's Long Road quake knocks everyone prone into it. Prone in deep mud is helpless for a round.
- **From across the field:** everyone is on the ground except the barefoot elf.

## Edwyn + Aerendyl — *Nothing Leaves the Hold*
- Edwyn's *dimensional anchor* goes on whatever Aerendyl is holding. It can't teleport, blink, plane shift or gate out of Long Road's grip.
- **From across the field:** a teleport flash that goes nowhere.

# 3. RELEASE PAIRINGS BETWEEN CAPTAINS (order Lirien's)

## Quavein + Mercy — *Lightning in the Reef*
- Each of Quavein's four bolts a round that strikes Mercy's black water hits **every creature in the sphere** for 10d6 (Reflex DC 26 half).
- Mercy, the moray, is immune.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 10d burning becomes Area 33 yd when it hits the Reef (the user's ally excluded).
- **From across the field:** the black sphere lights up from inside, four times a heartbeat, and goes dark.

## Tarvash + Aerendyl — *The Gatehouse Drops*
- Aerendyl lifts something huge: a gatehouse, a giant, a moored ship.
- Tarvash stoops onto it. A held target can't dodge: it takes the full 20d6 (no save) plus both blades as criticals. Aerendyl lets go at the moment of impact.
- **From across the field:** an elf holding a building over his head, then the building comes apart.

## Hadda + Brunna — *Iron in the Silk*
- Brunna's iron storm falls into a space strung with force threads. Every spike that strikes a strand stops there, and the room becomes a three-dimensional thornbush.
- Pinning works in mid-air: a creature can be pinned to a strand as well as to a wall.
- **From across the field:** a room full of spikes hanging in the air.

## Ysmay + Marit — *Hounds in the Dark*
- The Night Hunt's darkness is total, and the hounds hunt by scent.
- Inside the dark only Ysmay and Marit's forty can find anything. Every hound's bite counts as from behind for Ysmay's four strikes.
- **From across the field:** nothing, then barking, then nothing again.

# 4. THE RIVAL WEB (order Arik's; the dice made these)

These pairs work perfectly together or ruin each other, and in each case they'd rather not.

## Naevys + Kesh — *The Drive* (each counts the other as a rival)
- **Perfect fit.** The Hunt drives a whole line straight into Kesh's ten paces. Everything that survives the trample arrives inside his automatic sickle ring, already prone and frightened.
- **Why it never starts:** they're the two fastest promotions in the host, and each thinks the other was promoted too fast. Neither will call the release first. Arik has to give the order.
- **GURPS:** Knockdown from the Cone delivers targets into the Damage Aura with no defence.

## Quavein + Naevys — *Sunburst* (Quavein clashes with Naevys)
- **They ruin each other.** Naevys's best spell is *sunburst*. Quavein is drow: surface-hardened, but every sunburst near him leaves him dazzled.
- In the Gale, her sunburst lights the storm white and reveals everything Quavein's lightning was going to find first.
- **The quarrel in miniature:** the bare face against the black book.

## Naevys + Ysmay — *The Light That Doesn't Arrive*
- Naevys's *sunburst* cast into the Night Hunt is **swallowed**: no light pierces Ysmay's dark.
- Her strongest spell does nothing, and Ysmay notices whose spell it was. This is not on the rival web yet. It's how it would get there.

## Brunna and everyone — *Who Steals the Larder*
Four captains clash with Brunna. Each of their releases takes her pinned away before she can choose:
- **Tarvash:** Stoop craters collapse the walls she pinned people to, and the pinned fall free.
- **Naevys:** the Hunt rips the pinned off the walls (6d6 each, free).
- **Marit:** the hounds tear the pinned down and drag them off. Brunna and Marit clash both ways, so this one is personal.
- **Ysmay:** in the Night Hunt, Brunna's darkvision fails and Ysmay kills her catches before Brunna can see them.
- **The point:** the host's lightning rod in a single fight. Nobody lets the Shrike finish.

## Marit + Aerendyl — *The One Who Can Find Him*
- Aerendyl walks so the Shadow Gate network never sees him. Old Teeth is the one release that could drag him somewhere he hadn't decided to go: the quarry clause beats *dimensional anchor* and walls alike.
- He knows it. She knows he knows it. Their combination is not a tactic; it's a threat.

## Dace + Marit — *Forty Hounds* (Dace clashes with Marit)
- The Strike Before strikes **every enemy within 60 ft** each round. If Marit's hounds are ever counted as enemies, he destroys her pack in two rounds.
- He is the one captain who can kill Old Teeth's dogs, and they both know it.

## Hadda + Tarvash — *The Web Over the Courtyard* (Hadda clashes with Tarvash)
- A Stoop through the lit web tears the strands out of the crater, and every creature bound there falls free.
- Her web is the one place in the host he can't land cleanly. His dive is the one thing that cuts her web.

## Aerendyl + Ilvaera — *The Volunteer Finishes It* (Ilvaera clashes with Aerendyl)
- Whatever Aerendyl is holding, deciding what breaks first, is a target Ilvaera's darts will finish before he decides. She volunteers the kill.
- He walks away from fights he's won. She takes away the choice.

## Kesh + Mercy, Kesh + Zaheda — *The Man They Can't Hold* (both clash with Kesh)
- Kesh's *freedom of movement* (his own spell) makes him immune to Mercy's Hooked and to the Reef's slowing, and lets him run straight across Zaheda's ink.
- He is the one captain their releases cannot catch. That's why they don't like him.
