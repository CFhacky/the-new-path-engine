## The brief (Chad, 5 Oct 2026)
- **Something like the original Gotei 13.** The founding-era captains were a band of individually monstrous killers. Each was a law unto themselves and peerless in their own art, and they were held together by loyalty to one head rather than by uniform discipline.
- **What that means here.** Every captain is a named apex practitioner with a personal style and a reputation that arrives before they do. They are dangerous to stand next to and a little lawless, and their devotion runs to Arik and the Veil rather than to regulations.
- **"Menagerie" means no two are the same kind of animal.**
- **Monsters at every range (Chad, at the scale decision).** A captain who shows a ranged weapon carries no handicap in melee. "The point is all these folks are monsters — demons. I mean that metaphorically." That ruling governs every kit below. Nobody in this host has a soft body.

## Common to the host
- **The gloves.** Black silk with silver threading at the knuckles, worn to the elbow (Blue Hour). "That was the uniform."
- **The emblem.** Each captain's animal is worked into the silver threading of the left glove cuff. It shows only at arm's length, which is Veil austerity applied to rank. The division's lieutenant and triangles carry the same animal in plain thread.
- **Arrival.** They come by Shadow Jump, which needs Gray tier or higher. All eighty operate on the Veil operative kit at Gray or above, and a captain's class is layered over that base (combined arms, as with the Hand).
- **Cells of three in a triangle. Silence.** The only sound at Tethford was a sorcerer's foci clicking.
- **Divisions.** Each captain's following is a division numbered by seat (First through Thirteenth). "Seat" here means division order only. It is not the Espada usage on Mazikeen's page.

## The captains

Each entry gives the look head to toe, the art, the signature piece, the signature technique, the tell, the lawless streak, and what people say. **The signature pieces are authored as looks only.** Their mechanics go through loot-engine and the Affix Registry. **The signature techniques are written as fiction.** Their mechanics are resolved in the stat phase on the fused engine.

### I — Quavein Orlzynn, "the Bursar"
*Cleric 17 · drow, male, seasoned · Raven · Phantom (Bloodaxe Nordic)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-eight, tall for a drow and narrow through the shoulders. His skin is the matte black of wet slate, and his white hair is cropped to a quarter-inch so the scalp shows through. A ledger-callus sits on the right middle finger. Over the suit he wears a long surface-cut coat of charcoal wool with nothing in the pockets but a pencil stub. A raven skull the size of a walnut hangs from a cord at his throat. It carries no god's mark.
- **Art.** He is the war-priest who walks into the room first. He heals nobody but himself and whoever he decides is worth the spell. At contact he swings a heavy flanged mace whose reverse face is a hooked steel beak. At range he speaks a prayer that stops hearts. He fights the same way at either distance: a slow, careful man who arrives at the end of every exchange already knowing what it cost.
- **Signature piece.** *The beak-mace:* an iron haft wrapped in raven-black cord, with a flanged head and a hooked reverse spike. The spike's tip is polished bright from use and is the only bright metal on him.
- **Signature technique: Closing the Account.** He names a target's debt aloud, in a level voice, and then collects it with the beak. Witnesses remember the sentence better than the blow.
- **Tell.** A cord of black wooden beads threads through his left hand, and he moves one bead with his thumb for each thing he notices. *Click.* *Click.*
- **Lawless streak.** He runs a private side-ledger of operations, and Lirien learns of them afterwards. The black book records what each operation cost the Veil and what it took from the other side. Every page balances.
- **What people say.** "Quavein's book closes on you."

### II — Hadda Krell, "Spider-Hadda"
*Wizard 16 · half-orc, female, seasoned · Spider · Phantom, mage variant (Forgedeep dwarven)*
- **Look.** Six feet, heavy-boned, with grey-green skin crazed by fine white scar lines on both forearms: duelling scars, dozens of them. Her tusks are filed flat and capped in silver. Iron-grey hair hangs to her waist in a single rope plait. Wire-rimmed spectacles sit on a broken nose. Her spellbook travels in a riveted steel case at the right hip, chained to the belt.
- **Art.** She is a battle-wizard who fights at knife range. The left hand casts. The right holds a long-handled punching dagger with a triangular blade. She opens by filling the room with force and closes with the dagger, with no gap in between where a swordsman could get comfortable.
- **Signature piece.** *The punch-dagger:* an H-grip of blackened steel with a blade like a narrow trowel, and fine guilloche lines on the cross-bars that follow the force of a thrust.
- **Signature technique: the Silk.** In her first breath of a fight she strings fine threads of force across the space, invisible until they glint. From then on every step her opponent takes runs into a line she has already drawn.
- **Tell.** She takes the spectacles off, folds them and pockets them before she kills. People who know her watch her hands for that.
- **Lawless streak.** She duels for sport: Legion officers, hedge-mages, travelling swordsmen, anyone who will take the challenge. Her duels end at first blood, by her choice and only her choice.
- **What people say.** "If Hadda's taking her glasses off, leave."

### III — Tarvash Oruk, "the Press-Gang"
*Fighter 17 · half-orc, male, prime · Hawk · Stalker (Calishite-Amnian)*
- **Look.** Six-foot-four, raised in Calimshan, and he speaks Alzhedo by preference. His head is shaved. A hawk's flight-feather is tattooed from the brow over the crown to the nape, each barb inked separately. His skin is olive-grey and his eyes are amber. Gold koftgari in Aljibah script runs around the suit's collar: one verse, a hunting line, the only gold he allows himself.
- **Art.** He was a horse-archer before he was anything else. He shoots a recurve of horn and sinew faster than most men can draw a sword, and he fights in melee with a pair of short heavy scimitars, one in each hand. He goes high and comes down. Rooftops, battlements and balconies are where he starts every fight.
- **Signature piece.** *The paired scimitars:* broad at the tip, Calishite-curved, with grips wrapped in hawk-brown leather and the fullers inlaid with gold wire.
- **Signature technique: the Stoop.** He Shadow Jumps to a high shadow, then drops. Both blades land on the same target at the bottom of the fall.
- **Tell.** A hawk's two-note call whistled through his teeth, so soft it reads as a breath.
- **Lawless streak.** He recruits without clearance. He finds people (a pit fighter, a deserter, a girl who climbs better than anyone he has seen) and brings them in, and Lirien gets the paperwork afterwards. His one triangle is three of his own finds.
- **What people say.** "Tarvash looked at you twice? Pack a bag."

### IV — Aerendyl Ostahr, "Long-Road"
*Monk 16 · elf (moon), male, prime · Bear · Stalker (Forgedeep dwarven)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-ten and broad for an elf, with heavy shoulders, a thick neck and big flat hands. His head is shaved. Both ears are notched at the tips from old fights. His skin is pale with a blue undertone. He wears the suit with the sabatons cut away and goes barefoot on any ground. The soles of his feet are horn.
- **Art.** Unarmed, and he grapples. He closes, takes hold, and breaks whatever he is holding. At range he throws anything to hand (a stone, a mug, a broken chair leg) with enough force to cave a helm.
- **Signature piece.** *The prayer-cord:* a length of brown knotted rope wound round the right forearm, with a bear's claw tied at the end. He uses it as a garrotte.
- **Signature technique: the Hold.** He takes a grip that cannot be broken, then decides what breaks first.
- **Tell.** He hums one low continuous note while walking, steady as a millwheel.
- **Lawless streak.** He walks rather than gate-jumping, so the Shadow Gate network never logs him. He uses his own Shadow Jump and nothing of the lattice. Lirien cannot see him in the network ledger, and he still arrives on time.
- **What people say.** "Long-Road doesn't come through the gates. He comes through the door."

### V — Kesh Durrow, "Ten-Paces"
*Ranger 16 (archer-skirmisher) · half-orc, male, young · Mantis · Stalker (Forgedeep dwarven)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-eleven, lean as a whip, with long arms and grey-green skin. His left eye is milky white from a childhood burn, and he is the best shot in the host anyway. His black hair is tied in a short tail. A sickle-knife is strapped reversed along each forearm, edges out. On his back he carries a heavy recurve of laminated horn and darkwood.
- **Art.** He kills at ten paces either way. Inside ten paces he uses the sickles: a hook, a cut, a second hook. Outside ten he uses the bow. The handover between the two is seamless, and he is equally dangerous at three paces and at three hundred (Chad's ruling made flesh).
- **Signature piece.** *The mantis-sickles:* short inward-curved blades with ring pommels, worn reversed so the blade lies along the forearm and hooks out on a turn of the wrist.
- **Signature technique: the Fold.** He looses an arrow at contact range, point-blank up under the chin, while the off-hand sickle hooks the target's arm out of the way.
- **Tell.** He stands with his forearms folded up across his chest, sickles flat against his arms, perfectly still.
- **Lawless streak.** He answers to Arik, and only grudgingly to Lirien's staff. Her deputies have learned to phrase his orders as Arik's.
- **What people say.** "Count your distance from Kesh. Then stop counting."

### VI — Marit Hollowell, "Old Teeth"
*Rogue / Assassin 17 · human, female, seasoned · Hound · Strider (pure Veil austerity)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-five, square, with a dockworker's build and a weathered face. Her grey hair is chopped at the jaw, her knuckles are cracked, and her eyes are pale grey-blue. She wears a long oilcloth coat over the plainest suit in the host. Inside the coat's lining hangs a string of drilled human teeth, long enough to wrap her waist twice.
- **Art.** A tracker-assassin. She follows a person for days or weeks, through a city or across a province, and learns how they sleep, where they piss and who they love. Then she kills them once, with a butcher's boning knife or a hand-crossbow bolt from forty yards. She is lethal at either distance.
- **Signature piece.** *The boning knife:* a narrow, flexible six-inch blade on a plain beech handle, honed so often that it has worn into a slight crescent.
- **Signature technique: the Long Run.** She keeps after a target until she has studied them completely, then kills with a single stroke.
- **Tell.** She sniffs the air openly when she enters a room, like a dog at a door.
- **Lawless streak.** She keeps a trophy from every kill: one tooth, drilled and strung. It is evidence on a cord, and a security breach the Veil has tolerated for years.
- **What people say.** "Old Teeth is following someone. Pray it's not you."

### VII — Naevys Tolúrin, "the Barefaced"
*Sorcerer 16 · elf (sun), female, prime · Boar · Phantom, mage variant (pure Veil austerity)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-seven and heavy-set for an elf, with deep gold skin. Her copper hair is shaved at the sides, and the rest stands in a stiff roach dyed darker at the tips. A crescent boar tusk hangs at her throat on a bronze wire. She wears no hood, mask or veil. Her face is bare in the field.
- **Art.** A sorcerer of raw force who casts at the distance of a punch. She leads from the front of her division, the biggest in the host. When the spells are spent she uses a short boar-spear with a crossbar below the head, and uses it well. Snow melts on her (Blue Hour).
- **Signature piece.** *The boar-spear:* a short ash haft wrapped in boar hide, with a broad leaf head and an iron crossbar so a charging thing stops on the blade instead of running up the shaft.
- **Signature technique: the Charge.** She Shadow Jumps into the middle of the enemy line and lets go of everything she is holding.
- **Tell.** She laughs, a short bark, right before she commits.
- **Lawless streak.** She shows her face in the field, and Lirien loathes it. Her operatives follow her for that reason: they know whose face they are dying behind.
- **What people say.** "The Barefaced doesn't hide. You'll have seen her coming. It won't help."

### VIII — Brunna Coldhollow, "the Shrike"
*Swordsage 16 · dwarf, female, prime · Shrike · no suit (Veil kit only)*
- **Look.** Four-foot-three and built like a forge anvil. She has black side-whiskers braided tight and pinned with iron spikes, a flat nose broken twice, and dark brown eyes set deep. Over the Veil kit she wears a sleeveless hauberk of blackened leather. A belt of finger-long iron spikes, forty of them in loops, crosses her chest.
- **Art.** A swordsage of the shadow-hand and setting-sun disciplines. She fights with a pair of narrow thrusting blades, and she throws the spikes underhand at any range with the same aim. She works close and she works pinned.
- **Signature piece.** *The tucks:* two narrow, stiff thrusting swords, square in section and needle-pointed, with plain iron guards. They carry the Coldhollow clan mark on the ricasso.
- **Signature technique: the Larder.** She pins a target in place with spikes through the hands and feet, against a door, a wall or a tabletop, and finishes at leisure.
- **Tell.** She rolls an iron spike across her knuckles: thumb, index, middle, ring, and back.
- **Lawless streak.** When told to take someone alive, she kills them if she judges it cleaner. Lirien has stopped giving her capture jobs. Brunna considers that a reasonable outcome.
- **Why no suit.** "A suit is a cage with a door." The roll gave none, and she would have refused one.
- **What people say.** "The Shrike leaves them up where you can see them."

### IX — Mercy Vantreth, "Quiet Mercy"
*Barbarian 16 · tiefling, male, prime · Moray · Phantom (Forgedeep dwarven)*
- **Look.** Six-foot-six, with charcoal skin and smooth ridged horns swept flat back along the skull. His eyes are pale yellow with no visible white. His jaw is wide, and the teeth behind it are a second row too many. Two narrow repoussé panels on the suit's chest show eels coiled in a reef, Forgedeep relief work. He does not wear a helm.
- **Art.** A silent berserker. When the rage takes him his mouth opens wide and no sound comes out. He fights with a short heavy cleaver in the right hand and an iron gaff hook in the left, and at range he throws weighted javelins that go through shields.
- **Signature piece.** *The gaff:* a hooked iron bar on an ash grip with a barbed point, the kind fishermen use to land something big. He has used it to haul men off walls.
- **Signature technique: the Second Jaw.** The first blow lands, the gaff hooks behind the knee or under the collarbone, and he drags the target into the second blow.
- **Tell.** He swallows, twice, before violence.
- **Lawless streak.** He keeps a family the Veil does not know about: a wife and two children in a tanners' lane in Neverwinter. That makes him the softest breach in the host, carried by its hardest man.
- **What people say.** "Mercy doesn't make a sound. That's the only mercy in it."

### X — Zaheda Qorrin, "Ink-Tiger"
*Druid 16 · human (Calishite), female, prime · Tiger · Stalker (Calishite-Amnian)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-nine and long-limbed, with deep brown skin and black hair bound under a wrapped indigo turban. Her fingertips are permanently stained with ink. Over the right shoulder and down the back run four parallel scars, the marks of the animal that taught her its shape. Aljibah verse-bands in fine gold koftgari run across the suit's chest.
- **Art.** A druid who goes to war as a tiger: four hundred pounds of striped muscle that comes out of shadow at a dead run. In her own skin she uses a scimitar and a sling, and she is lethal at either range and in either body.
- **Signature piece.** *The notebook and reed pen:* a palm-sized book of oiled paper in a tiger-hide cover, and a cut reed pen she sharpens with her teeth.
- **Signature technique: the Margin.** She writes the kill in her notebook before she does it: time, place, method. Then she goes and makes the entry true.
- **Tell.** She writes. Always, in Aljibah script, in the margins of whatever is under her hand.
- **Lawless streak.** She writes everything down. Lirien has asked her to stop twice. A captured notebook would burn half the Veil, if anyone could read Zaheda's private cipher.
- **What people say.** "If you're in Ink-Tiger's book, you're already dead. You just haven't read the page."

### XI — Ysmay Corran, "Ysmay Who Said No"
*Warblade 16 · half-elf, female, old · Owl · Phantom (pure Veil austerity)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-six and upright as a fence post. Her white hair is cropped to the skull, her eyes are deep-set and yellow-brown, and liver spots cover the backs of her hands. Her face is all bone and line. She wears a hand-and-a-half sword on her back in a plain black scabbard. Quavein has more years behind him, but she wears hers, and she is the captain the others go quiet around.
- **Art.** A warblade of the iron-heart and diamond-mind disciplines. Each fight takes her a single cut, two at most, and she makes it from directly above or directly behind. She throws a knife left-handed at forty feet without looking at it.
- **Signature piece.** *The old sword:* a hand-and-a-half blade with a long ricasso, its grip rewrapped so many times that the wrapping is thicker than the tang. The edge has been ground back by a full finger's width over the decades.
- **Signature technique: the Night Hunt.** In darkness and silence she arrives as one cut from above, the way an owl takes a mouse.
- **Tell.** She turns her head without turning her body.
- **Lawless streak.** She refused a direct order from Lirien once, and lived. What the order was is undisclosed: a hook for Chad.
- **What people say.** "Ysmay said no to the Director. Think about that, and then don't say anything to Ysmay."

### XII — Ilvaera Doun, "the Volunteer"
*Duskblade 17 · elf (wood), female, seasoned · Manticore · Stalker (Forgedeep dwarven)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-four and slight, with warm brown skin and chestnut hair in a single heavy braid wrapped with steel wire. Freckles cover her nose and cheekbones. Her eyes are green and kind, which is what people remember afterwards. At the hip she carries a quiver of short steel darts, and a curved long sword in a scabbard of oiled wood.
- **Art.** A duskblade: spell and steel in one stroke. She channels spells down the sword so every cut carries one. At range she throws the darts in fans, each charged with lightning. She is equally deadly at a sword's length and at a dart's throw.
- **Signature piece.** *The manticore darts:* a quiver of twenty steel spikes, each fletched with a single black feather and etched along the shaft with Forgedeep guilloche.
- **Signature technique: Tail Volley.** A fan of six darts, each carrying a shock, thrown from hiding into a group before they know she is there.
- **Tell.** She apologises to her targets in Elvish, quietly, and means it.
- **Lawless streak.** She takes work Arik has not asked for, on Arik's behalf. She decides who Arik would want dead and then sees to it. Nobody knows how many there have been.
- **What people say.** "The Volunteer's sorry. You're still dead."

### XIII — Dace Tolland, "Any-Comer"
*Psychic Warrior 16 · human, male, prime · Adder · Stalker (Metropolitan, urushi itself Phantom-gated)*
- **Look.** Five-foot-nine, wiry, and Waterdhavian, with a clean-shaved face and dark close-cropped hair. A black-and-tan adder is tattooed coiling once around his throat, its head under the left ear. His eyes are pale grey and he rarely blinks. The suit carries vermilion lacquer in the urushi colour. True urushi is Phantom-tier, so his is ordinary lacquer in the Metropolitan palette.
- **Art.** A psychic warrior whose sense of what you are about to do arrives before you have decided it. He fights with a short broad-bladed spear in close and throwing knives at range, and he is the same man at both distances.
- **Signature piece.** *The short spear:* a five-foot ash haft with a leaf blade and a red-lacquered socket, with a coiled adder scorched into the wood below the head.
- **Signature technique: the Strike Before.** He moves into the space where your attack is about to go, and is already there when it arrives.
- **Tell.** He blinks slowly, once, when he has read you.
- **Lawless streak.** He accepts any challenge, from anyone, anywhere: in a tavern, on a pier, at a wedding. He is a public brawler serving in a covert service.
- **What people say.** "Any-Comer will fight you. That's the problem."

  - *"You owe the Veil three nights' sleep. I'm collecting one."*

- **What he wants.** The priestess who ended his house, dead. Her name is the first entry in the black book, and the only one that has never balanced. (Her name and house are left open for Chad. They touch Underdark canon.)

- **Secret (hook).** The back of the black book holds a second ledger, in drow script: what the office has given and what it is owed. He is keeping accounts for a god who does not yet know he is one.

## Captains — fused-engine stat blocks (5 Oct 2026)
Each block follows the Wardsinger format: a 3.5e block with fused active defenses, then a GURPS 4e line. The arithmetic is done by `build_menagerie_stats.py` from `menagerie_captains_data.json`, with no dice involved.

**Working rules (flagged; Chad to confirm or overrule):**
- **R1. Shadow Jump is a Captain's Writ, granted through Nightwind attunement** (the enhancement Project Shadow Gate runs on): Su, 80 ft/day, shadow to shadow.
  - *Why:* in 3.5e, Shadow Jump comes from Shadowdancer. That class needs 10 ranks of Hide, which non-rogues can't reach before level 17, and the levels would cut every captain below their rolled level in their own art.
  - *The alternative:* Shadowdancer 4 counted inside the rolled level.
- **R2. Stride AC.** The suit's AC replaces 10 + armor. The pilot adds their pre-suit Dex modifier, deflection, natural armor and class AC bonuses.
  - Touch AC = 10 + effective Dex modifier + deflection + class bonuses.
  - Flat-footed AC = AC − pre-suit Dex modifier.
- **R3. Suit attack count.** When a suit's base attacks (Strider 3, Stalker 4, Phantom 5) exceed the attacks from BAB, the extra attacks are made at the lowest attack bonus.
- **R4. Suit ability boosts.** The suit's Str, Dex and Con boosts count for attack, damage, saves, initiative and HP.
- **R5. The gloves.** The gloves are the Evening's Edge. Their armor bonus doesn't stack with a suit, but the claws stay usable as a backup weapon.
- **R6. Signature pieces.** These are masterwork only (+1). Enhancements and affixes go through loot-engine.
- **R7. Converting to GURPS.**
  - Attribute = 10 + (score before items and suit − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down.
  - Dodge = Basic Speed (rounded down) + 3, +1 for Combat Reflexes, + the suit's bonus.
  - Parry = weapon skill ÷ 2 (rounded down) + 3, +1 for Combat Reflexes, + the suit's bonus.
  - CP are approximate (≈30 × level), at campaign scale.
- **Standard kit:** the Veil loadout, cloak of resistance +4 and ring of protection +3.
  - **Throat slot:** one item only, either a periapt of Wisdom or an amulet (health, or natural armor +2 if the slot is otherwise free).
  - **Hands slot:** this is the Evening's Edge itself, so any Dex enhancement is woven into a captain's-grade pair.
  - *(Corrected in the same batch, before use: Quavein's Con, HP and Fort were first posted with a periapt and an amulet sharing the throat slot.)*

### The socket layer (rolled 5 Oct 2026)
**Method.**
- **Commissioned sockets.** The signature pieces are masterwork and non-magical, so the Runeword Crafting System allows sockets to be commissioned in them.
- **Rolled on the loot-engine tables.** Count and type were rolled on the Section 9 tables. Every die is a `loot_roll.py roll` call (Python `secrets`), with raw output in `menagerie_socket_rolls.txt`.
- **Gem sockets.** Each one rolled d2: 1 = a stat gem (Section 25, d7 in printed order, as for the Hand), 2 = a **spell gem**.
- **Rune sockets.** These rolled the high-rune d10 (levels 13–17).
- **Seal, charm, shard and strange sockets** stay empty until the signature-item pass. That pass **skips its own socket roll** for these pieces.
- **Tiers.** Captains are Tier 1. Lieutenants are Tier 1, except Osmund (CR 12), who is Tier 2.

**Spell gem (Reservoir row, canon).** A gem holds one stored spell of up to 7th level at Tier 1, or 5th at Tier 2.
- **Charging.** A caster charges it by casting the spell into the gem (SRD ring of spell storing convention). The stored spell keeps that caster's level and DC.
- **Releasing it.** The bearer releases it as a standard action.
- **Socketed in a weapon.** A spell that targets a creature may instead be released on a hit, as a free action (SRD spell storing convention). The 3rd-level cap of spell storing is lifted by the Reservoir tier.
- **Cost.** UDRP +1 per socket, plus the stored spell's casting.
