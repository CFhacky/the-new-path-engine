## Readings that need Chad's ruling

1. **Empty-socket contents (proposed; the rolls stand only if you confirm).** loot-engine Section 25 tables gems and runes but leaves seal, charm, shard and strange sockets as stubs. The proposal, stated before the dice:
   - **Seal** = a ward or binding: d2 picks the Defensive or Condition/Control pool, then a d100 row at the item's tier.
   - **Charm** = a small passive: d2 picks Utility or Skill/Class, then a row one tier lower, never Greater.
   - **Shard** = the Summoning pool's *Elemental Shard* row at the item's tier (Huge elemental, 1/day). Element authored.
   - **Strange** = DM-authored geometry, seeded by one aspect-table draw.

   Results:
   - Quavein seal: **Stalwart +4**.
   - Naevys seal: **Aegis +5**.
   - Aerendyl charm: **Dimensional Pocket 100 lb**.
   - Strange sockets: Aerendyl **24J-6 Weave**, Dace **24G-6 Coiled Spring**, Rhun **24I-4 Revenant**.
   - Shards: Marit earth, Ashavel air.
2. **The division rule (affinity translation).** Companion- and minion-keyed draws on a bearer with no companion or minions on the sheet (Familiar Bond, Sympathetic Link, Horde Surge, Pack) apply to **one sworn operative of the bearer's own division**, designated at dawn and within 30 ft. Draws whose bearer does have one keep it: Kesh's animal companion, Quavein's summons, Osmund's raised dead, Edwyn's and Zaheda's summons.
3. **Arcane Amplification on a divine caster (Osmund)** reads as +2 to **divine** spell DCs, the Sacred Word row's wording at the same value.
4. **Wardbreaker on a fighter (Patience, Rhun).** It reads as a hand-off: a creature their weapon hits has its SR reduced by the Wardbreaker value against their allies' spells until the end of the bearer's next turn. They open the ward, and the casters finish it.
5. **Zaheda's notebook in tiger form.** Carried items meld when she wild-shapes, and RAW says melded items stop working. The proposal is that the notebook's powers live on the written page: an entry made before the shape-change keeps working while the book is melded. Without that ruling the notebook is a human-form item.
6. **The writ, the runes and the recall** are all confirmed (see *Rulings recorded*).

## 1. THE LAST COLUMN
Heavy mace, flanged, with a hooked reverse beak · Legendary · Tier 1 · Quavein Orlzynn

### D&D 3.5e Properties
- **+2 mighty cleaving heavy mace** (MW 14: +2 enhancement total and one mid-tier property, d12 = 9). 1d8+2. Mighty cleaving: one extra Cleave attempt per round.
- **Familiar Bond (T1, affinity translation):** the first creature Quavein summons or calls each day gains **+5 HP per caster level (+85 HP)**. His Water domain gives *elemental swarm*.
- **Armor Piercing (T1):** each hit ignores the first **10** points of hardness or DR.
- **Warding (T1):** **+4 deflection** bonus to AC. It replaces his ring of protection's +3, so the net change is +1.
- **Executioner (T1, GREATER):** **+6d6** damage against a target below half its HP (base 4d6, stepped by ladder 20B).
- **Temper 23H-8 Vital Strike (T1):** on a confirmed critical hit against a creature with a discernible anatomy, **+4d6**.
- **Aspect 24E-7 Siege (T1):** his melee attacks with the mace may be made against a target within **100 ft**. They become ranged attacks at his melee attack bonus and damage, with no range increments, and they provoke as ranged attacks. **Closing the Account** works at any distance in the room.
- **Sockets:** spell gems holding *destruction* and *heal* (round 4).
  - Seal: **Stalwart +4** resistance bonus on saves (proposed reading). This is redundant with his cloak of resistance +4 and adds nothing until the cloak slot is spent elsewhere. It frees that slot.
- CL 16. Market: +2 mighty cleaving heavy mace **18,312 gp** (+3 bonus-equivalent). The affix layer is UDRP-priced: **UDRP 33**.

### GURPS 4e Properties
- Fine mace. Masterwork band 13–16: +2 damage plus one Follow-Up Innate Attack, read as the Executioner rider.
- Armor Piercing: Armor Divisor (5).
- Warding: DR +4 (Gadget).
- Executioner: Innate Attack 4d+2 (Follow-Up; trigger: target below half HP, −20%).
- Vital Strike: Innate Attack 4d (trigger: critical against a living target).
- Siege: the mace's attacks may be Ranged (Acc 2, Range 33 yd), with the same skill.
- Familiar Bond: Ally (summoned) +85 HP.
- Limitations: Breakable (DR 6) −25%, Can Be Stolen (grab first) −10%.

### Lore Hook
An iron haft wrapped in raven-black cord. The flanged head is dull, and the beak's tip is the one bright point on the man. It was the bursar's mace of the minor house that raised him. When a rival bought the matron's death from a priestess, the house's last ledger was left open on the counting-table with its final column never totalled, and Quavein carried out the book and the mace together. Everything he has collected since has gone into that column. **Complication:** Siege makes the mace a weapon you cannot step back from, so his enemies learn to close with him instead. The priestess who ended his house would know the mace on sight. And the column will not balance until her name does.

## 7. COME ON THEN — Unique
Boar-spear: short ash haft in boar hide, broad leaf head, iron crossbar · Unique · Tier 1 band · Naevys Tolúrin

*Name source:* when Naevys was a girl riding with her grandmother's wild hunt, a boar came out of the brake at her. The huntsman put this spear in her hands and said two words to the child: "Come on, then." She laughed. She still laughs before she commits.

### D&D 3.5e Properties
- **+2 shortspear.** 1d6+2.
- **Set (built-in).** When Naevys arrives by Shadow Jump or teleportation, she may set the spear as a free action. While it is set and until she moves:
  - She cannot be moved against her will: bull rush, overrun, trip and grapple-moves fail.
  - Every creature that moves into her reach provokes an attack of opportunity from the spear.
  - A hit deals double damage (set against a charge, applied to any approach) and ends that creature's movement.
- **The Crossbar.** A creature hit by the set spear is caught on the crossbar. It cannot move away from her without a DC 25 Strength check (a move action). She casts with her free hand without provoking from any creature on the crossbar.
- **The Boar Keeps Coming (aspect-grade).** Once per day, if she is reduced to 0 HP or below while the spear is set, she stays conscious and acting until the end of her next turn as if at 1 HP. Damage in that window is recorded, and when the window ends she falls at the true total, dead if it is past −10.
- **The win condition:** the Charge. She Shadow Jumps into the line, sets the spear, and casts her maximised area spell centred on herself. Every enemy that tries to close is stopped on the blade.
- **Cost and limit.** The spear belongs to the hunt and works only while her face is bare. If she is hidden from the world's sight for a full day (her bargain), the spear goes first: it is gone at dusk, and the hunt's horn sounds where she stands. Fey of the hunt feel it within a mile.
- **Sockets:** a **Sapphire** (+4 AC, round 4) and a seal holding **Aegis +5** AC against attacks of opportunity (proposed reading). She walks into the line and is hard to punish for it.
- CL 13. Market: +2 base with Set (+2) and the Crossbar (+1) is +5 bonus-equivalent, 50,000 gp. The Boar Keeps Coming is a 4th-level effect at CL 13, 1/day, as a further property at 50%: 9,360 gp. Plus 301 gp. **≈ 59,661 gp.** **UDRP 27.**

### GURPS 4e Properties
- Fine spear.
- Set: Immovable Stance, plus a Counterattack that ends movement on a hit (×2 damage).
- Crossbar: Binding (Sticky, ST 15 to break, Follow-Up).
- The Boar Keeps Coming: Unkillable 1 (1/day; only while the spear is set).
- Limitation: Accessibility (only while barefaced) −10%. The disappearance is a story effect, not a limitation.

### Lore Hook
The boar-hide on the haft is from the boar of that morning, and the crossbar still carries its tusk-marks. The house of formal wizards that tried to train the fey out of her never saw the spear, because it does not stay in a house with a roof. The hunt lent it to a girl it meant to come back for. **Complication:** Lirien loathes the bare face, and the spear is why Naevys will never cover it. ## Errata — 6 Oct 2026: the plus-damage audit (Chad flagged the inconsistency; the cards above are superseded only on these points)

**How the plus is meant to work.** A rolled Legendary's enhancement comes from its masterwork d20 on table 21A, and on nothing else:

| Masterwork d20 | 3.5e enhancement | GURPS damage |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | none (+1 to one secondary property) | +1 to skill or Acc only |
| 5–8 | +1 | +1 |
| 9–12 | +1 and a minor property | +1, Min ST −1 |
| 13–16 | +2 and a mid-tier property | +2 and one Follow-Up add-on |
| 17–20 | +3 and a major property | +2 and a major advantage |

Read that way, the Legendaries are: Marit (5) +1, Osmund (9) +1, Edwyn (12) +1, Quavein (14) +2, Hadda (17) +3, Kesh (17) +3, Brunna (20) +3, **Rhun (1) none**. The spread from +1 to +3 is the dice; the error was Rhun.

1. **Rhun Talbridge, *The Relief* (3.5e), corrected.** Masterwork 1 gives **no enhancement**. The card wrongly made it a +1 halberd.
   - The pollaxe is a **masterwork halberd**: R6 +1 to attack, no enhancement bonus.
   - The secondary property is **+1 on trip attempts with the hook**.
   - Damage **1d10** (not 1d10+1). Market **310 gp** (not 2,310).
   - Errata line: *The Relief* +23/+18/+13/+13, **1d10+15** +5d6, 19–20/×3. The earlier line's 1d10+16 is withdrawn.
   - UDRP is unchanged at 34: masterwork below 5 always added 0.
2. **GURPS Greater dice, corrected.** The 3.5e ladder (20B) multiplies the dice by about 1.5: 3d6 → 5d6, 4d6 → 6d6. The GURPS columns must step the same way, from the T1 GURPS value:
   - **Quavein, Executioner (Greater):** T1 4d becomes **6d** (not 4d+2).
   - **Rhun, Wounding (Greater):** T1 2d becomes **3d** (not 3d+2).
3. **Quavein, *The Last Column* (GURPS), corrected.** The band 13–16 add-on belongs to the masterwork's own mid-tier property, *mighty cleaving*, not to Executioner. Executioner already has its own Innate Attack line, so the card counted it twice.
   - The GURPS line reads: **+2 damage; Extra Attack 1 (trigger: a foe in reach is downed), as mighty cleaving.**

Checked and consistent, no change:
- The Legendaries: Hadda, Kesh, Marit, Brunna, Osmund, Edwyn. Faelith's shield enhancement goes to AC; the bash takes the bashing property's own +1.
- The Uniques: Naevys +2, Mercy +2, Kerra +2, Ilvaera +1.
- The Rares, which are masterwork only.
