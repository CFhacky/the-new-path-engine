# The Menagerie — Releases (authored, 6 Oct 2026)

> **What this is.** Chad's ruling: the rolled signature items stay as each weapon's **sealed state**. On top of them, every bearer has a **release**: one moment a day when the weapon and the monster carrying it stop pretending to be a weapon and a soldier. These are spectacle, written to break formations and buildings. They are authored, not rolled. **Nothing here has happened in play.**

## The frame
- **Bound, not loot.** A release lives in the bond between the bearer and the piece. Kill the bearer and you take a sealed item off the body; the release dies with them. It sits outside UDRP, the reward budget and the item manifests.
- **Captains:** one full release a day.
  - A swift action to call; it lasts **10 rounds (1 minute)**. Supernatural (Su).
  - Save DC **26**. *Antimagic* suppresses it, but it does not end.
- **Lieutenants:** one lesser release a day, about a quarter the size.
  - A swift action to call; it lasts **3 rounds**. Supernatural (Su).
  - Save DC **21**. Smaller ground, smaller dice.
- **Light bookkeeping.** Each release has the image, what it does at the table, a GURPS line, and what is left afterwards.
- **GURPS throughout:** Limited Use 1/day, with the 3.5e radius converted at 1 yd per 3 ft.

# THE THIRTEEN

## I. Quavein Orlzynn — *The Gale That Found Him*
He lifts the beak-mace, and the storm from the winter night he first saw open water walks into the room. The air goes black-green, the floor runs with seawater, and Quavein stands dry in the eye with his coat not moving.
- **The storm:** a **120-ft radius** hurricane centred on him, moving with him.
  - Anything Large or smaller that isn't anchored is thrown **1d4 × 10 ft** every round (Fort DC 26 negates), taking falling damage for the distance.
  - Roofs, tents, palisades and shutters are torn away in the first round.
- **Lightning:** **four bolts a round, 10d6 each** (Reflex DC 26 half), at any targets he points the mace at inside the storm.
- **Shut down inside:** ranged weapon attacks fail. Flight is impossible below Colossal. Casting needs a DC 26 Concentration check.
- **Reach:** the mace strikes anything inside the storm, at any distance.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 10d burning (lightning, ×4 per turn), Area 40 yd (moves with the user); Knockback; Obscure; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the room, and most of what was in it, is somewhere else. Salt crusts every surface for a month. People who survive it swear they heard a voice in the wind that was not his.

## II. Hadda Krell — *The Silk Pulled Tight*
She takes off the spectacles, folds them and pockets them. Then every thread she has strung all fight lights at once, and there are thousands of them.
- **The web:** every surface and open space within **100 ft** becomes her web of force.
  - Every creature in it is bound on the strands (Reflex DC 26 negates for one round; repeat the save each round).
- **The pull:** each round she closes her fist and the web draws tight.
  - **20d6 force** to every bound creature, with no save against the damage.
  - Stone, timber and iron in the web are cut into neat pieces.
- **Freely:** Hadda walks the strands as floor, wall or ceiling. She can open a gap in the web wherever she wants one.
- **GURPS:** Binding 20 (Engulfing, Area 33 yd), plus Innate Attack 6d×3 crushing (force), Follow-Up on bound targets; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the space looks as if a jeweller cut it. Survivors have fine white lines on every limb, like the scars on her forearms.

## III. Tarvash Oruk — *The Stoop*
A hawk's two-note whistle, and he is gone, 500 feet straight up, a dot against the sky.
- **Each round** he drops onto any point within a mile of where he left.
  - **Impact:** a **40-ft radius**, **20d6 bludgeoning** (Reflex DC 26 half). Anything built over the point collapses.
  - Whoever was directly beneath him takes **both scimitars as automatic critical hits**.
- **Back up:** he rises again at the end of his turn. Nothing on the ground can reach him between drops except ranged attacks at −10.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 20d crushing (Explosion 4) on landing, Super Jump / Flight (Limited: vertical); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** a courtyard of craters. The people he pressed into the Veil hear the whistle and go to ground before the first stone falls.

## IV. Aerendyl Ostahr — *Long Road*
The low note he hums while walking drops an octave, and for a hundred feet in every direction the ground answers to him.
- **Hold anything.** He can grapple anything of any size: a giant, a gatehouse, a moored ship, a stone dragon.
  - No opposed roll for anything Gargantuan or smaller.
  - Whatever he holds takes **15d6 a round** and cannot act except to try to escape (DC 36).
- **Quake.** The ground heaves with every step he takes: everyone else within 100 ft is knocked prone each round (Reflex DC 26 negates).
- **Throw.** As a move action he tears a section of wall, a cart or a column free and throws it 120 ft: **15d6** in a 20-ft radius.
- **GURPS:** Lifting ST +60 and Striking ST +40 (Grappling only), Innate Attack 15d crushing (Area 7 yd, thrown); Earthquake Area 33 yd; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** he walks out the way he came in, on foot, humming the old note. Nothing on the Shadow Gate network records that he was ever there.

## V. Kesh Durrow — *Ten Paces Both Ways*
He unfolds his arms. The milky eye sees for once, and everything inside a thousand feet stands at exactly the range he wants.
- **Outside ten paces** (each round): a volley that blots out the sun.
  - An **80-ft radius** anywhere within 1,000 ft takes **15d6 piercing** (Reflex DC 26 half). Shields and cover give no benefit.
- **Inside ten paces:** any enemy that starts its turn within 30 ft of him, or enters that space, is cut by both sickles automatically: **8d6** and hooked prone. There is no limit on the number.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 15d impaling (Area 27 yd, Range 333 yd, Rapid Fire), plus a Damage Aura of 8d cutting at 10 yd (with Knockdown); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the arrows stand in the ground like wheat. Each year Kesh stands ten paces from the harbour official who burned his eye, and does not draw. He knows what he could do.

## VI. Marit Hollowell — *Old Teeth*
She opens the oilcloth coat, and every tooth on the string becomes a hound.
- **The pack:** **forty** grey hounds the size of ponies pour out of the coat into the street.
  - Each one: AC 25; 40 HP; bite +22, 3d6 plus trip; scent; 80 ft speed.
- **Nothing hides from them.** The hounds find every hidden or invisible creature within 300 ft in the first round, and hunt them.
- **The quarry.** Marit names one creature she has ever tracked, and it does not need to be in sight. The hounds bring it to her, from anywhere on the plane, by the end of the release. It comes through walls and wards, and **dimensional anchor** does not stop it.
- **GURPS:** Ally Group (40 hound-shades, Summonable, 1/day) with Detect (Hidden) and Tracking 20; Warp (Accessibility: the named quarry, to her); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the teeth are back on the string, and there is one more. She never says whose.

## VII. Naevys Tolúrin — *The Hunt Rides Through*
She laughs her short bark, and her grandmother's wild hunt comes through her.
- **The charge:** a **300-ft line, 60 ft wide**, of spectral boar and horned riders.
  - **25d6 trample** to everything in the line (Reflex DC 26 half). Survivors are knocked flat and frightened.
  - Trees, palisades, siege lines and a formation of pikes all go down the same way.
- **Each round** she can send the hunt down a new line.
- **Naevys rides at the front.** That round nothing can touch her: attacks miss and spells fail.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 25d crushing (Cone 100 yd × 20 yd), Knockdown, Terror; Insubstantiality while charging; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the horn sounds for a long time. The fey remember who called them. Her bare face is the price, and she pays it laughing.

## VIII. Brunna Coldhollow — *The Larder*
She rolls an iron spike over her knuckles once, thumb to little finger, and throws it. It is not one spike when it lands.
- **The iron storm:** a **100-ft radius** fills with a storm of iron spikes.
  - **20d6 piercing** (Reflex DC 26 half).
  - Anyone who fails is **pinned** to the nearest wall, floor or tabletop by the hands and feet. Tearing free takes a DC 30 Strength check and 6d6 damage.
- **The ground stays thorned.** The whole area is difficult terrain. Each round another 10d6 storm falls wherever she points.
- **Brunna flies** (the tucks' Featherfall) and works the pinned at leisure, one at a time.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 20d impaling (Area 33 yd), Binding 20 (Unbreakable), Follow-Up; Flight; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** a field of iron thorns and what hangs on them. The shrike always leaves the larder where you can see it.

## IX. Mercy Vantreth — *The Reef*
He swallows twice. Then the sea arrives where there was no sea.
- **The water:** a **100-ft sphere** of black, cold sea water fills the space, even indoors or on a hilltop.
  - Everyone inside is underwater: they hold their breath, can't speak or cast verbal spells, and ranged weapons fail.
  - Fire goes out. Everything in the sphere is at −4 to attack and −10 ft to speed.
- **Mercy is the moray.** He swims 120 ft and makes a full attack every round against anyone in the water.
  - The gaff hauls each target into the cleaver.
  - Mal applies to everything he touches: nothing in the reef heals.
- **No sound** leaves the sphere. Nobody outside it hears anything.
- **GURPS:** Control Water (create, Area 33 yd) with Obscure (Hearing, total) and Mute on all within; Enhanced Move (Water) 3; Extra Attack 2; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the water is gone in a heartbeat, leaving wet stone, dead fish from no river anyone knows, and no witnesses. In a tanners'-lane house in Neverwinter, his eldest wakes from a dream of black water and does not cry out.

## X. Zaheda Qorrin — *The Page*
She opens the notebook and writes across the air. The ink keeps going, out of the book and across the ground, until the whole field is her margin.
- **The page:** a **200-ft radius** fills with Aljibah script in wet black ink.
  - It is difficult terrain for everyone but Zaheda and what she writes.
- **Tigers.** Each round she writes up to **four** names. A **dire tiger made of ink** rises at each named creature and fights it to the death.
  - Each tiger: AC 22, 120 HP, claws and bite +24 (2d6+12 each), pounce, rake.
- **Zaheda becomes the teacher:** a tiger the size of a house (Huge; Str 36; claws +30, 3d6+13 each; bite 4d6+13; rake).
  - The notebook does not meld; it opens on the ground beside her and keeps writing.
- **GURPS:** Ally Group (4 per turn, ink tigers, Summonable), Alternate Form (Huge tiger, ST 40); Obscure; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the ink dries to a dark stain that cannot be scrubbed out, in a script nobody else alive can read. Every name she wrote is in the book. They are crossed out.

## XI. Ysmay Corran — *The Night Hunt*
She closes her failing eyes. When she opens them the sun is gone.
- **The dark:** a **500-ft radius** of absolute, magical darkness and silence.
  - No light pierces it, divine light included, and nothing inside can be heard.
  - Ysmay sees perfectly in it, the way she always has.
- **Each round** she moves anywhere in the dark without passing through the space between, and strikes **four different creatures**, once each, from above or behind.
  - Every strike is an **automatic critical hit**: the old sword, ×2, with battle cunning.
- **The Covenant.** In the dark she does not bleed. Damage that would drop her below 1 HP is deferred until the release ends.
- **GURPS:** Darkness (Area 167 yd, Total) with Silence, Warp (Limited: within the darkness), Extra Attack 3 (Accessibility: from behind), Unkillable 2 for the duration; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the light comes back slowly, like a door opening. Whatever was owed comes due on her at once, and she pays it standing. She says one name each time, never the same one: the charges she was a heartbeat late for.

## XII. Ilvaera Doun — *The Tail*
She whispers the apology in Elvish once, to all of them at once, and means it. Then she throws the quiver.
- **The volley:** the twenty darts become **two thousand**.
  - A **150-ft cone** takes **12d6 piercing**, plus the touch spell she has channelled, delivered to **every creature hit**.
  - That is 5d6 more from *shocking grasp*, or *vampiric touch*, or *slow*, as she chooses.
- **Each round** she can throw again: a new cone and a new spell.
- **Darts stay in the wound.** Until they are pulled (a full-round action each), they give her a channelled touch spell's worth of reach on that target: she can deliver another touch spell to it at any range.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 12d impaling (Cone 50 yd, Rapid Fire 100+), with a Follow-Up spell delivered on every hit; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the darts come home to the quiver, all twenty of them, clean. The ground looks as if a manticore spent a week shedding there.

## XIII. Dace Tolland — *The Strike Before*
He blinks slowly, once, and he has already seen the next minute. He is already standing in it.
- **First, always.** For the duration Dace acts at the **top of every round and again at the bottom**, two full turns per round.
- **Everywhere they meant to go.** At the start of each round, before anyone acts, he strikes **every enemy within 60 ft** once with the spear.
  - Each hit lands Deep Impact (+4d6, resolved as a touch attack).
  - The adder's venom: **1d6 Con damage** (Fort DC 26 negates).
- **Nothing aimed at him lands.** Attacks against Dace miss unless the attacker beats DC 26 on a Will save first.
- **GURPS:** Altered Time Rate 1, Precognition (combat), Enhanced Defenses (all, +5); Rapid Strike against all foes within 20 yd with Follow-Up toxic; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the challengers lie where they were going to stand. He sees one heartbeat further than he did before, and Arik is in it.

# THE EIGHT (lesser releases: 3 rounds, DC 21)

## Lt I. Osmund Tarrow — *Funeral Smoke*
The censer swings once, and the pauper's-pit smoke pours out in a flood.
- A **40-ft cloud** of grey smoke: full concealment for everyone but Osmund.
- **6d6 negative energy** a round to every living creature inside (Fort DC 21 half).
- Anything that dies in the smoke **rises** at once as a zombie under his command, to a maximum of 4.
- **GURPS:** Obscure (Vision) Area 13 yd, Innate Attack 6d toxic (negative, Area), Death Knight Ally Group (max 4); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the smoke smells of a cheap funeral. Somewhere, three old women are pleased.

## Lt II. Kerra Lisle — *Ten Paces*
She draws a line in the dirt with the rapier and starts to count aloud.
- A **30-ft dueling circle** of force rises. Nothing enters or leaves for 3 rounds.
- On the third round she reaches "ten." Every enemy in the circle takes **10d6 force** (Will DC 21 half), and her principal gains a free attack against each of them.
- **GURPS:** Force Wall (Area 10 yd, Cage) with a delayed Innate Attack 10d crushing (force); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the line in the dirt stays. Duellists in Waterdeep have started to step around marks like it.

## Lt III. Wenna Sorrel — *Press-Gang*
She takes the reed out of her mouth and gives an order in a voice off a Luskan quay.
- A **40-ft burst**: every creature in it changes sides for 3 rounds (Will DC 21 negates; one save only).
- The pressed fight for the Veil with everything they have, and remember all of it afterwards.
- **GURPS:** Mind Control (Area 13 yd, Conditioning-free, 3 turns); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the pressed don't trust their own hands for weeks. Tarvash calls it recruiting.

## Lt IX. Faelith Ammarin — *The Wall Song*
She sings the wall-note, off-key on purpose, and the old company answers.
- A **60-ft line** of spectral elven tower shields rises beside her.
  - Allies behind it have total cover.
  - Anything that strikes the wall rebounds onto its attacker for **8d6** (no save).
- Faelith herself cannot be moved and heals **10 HP** a round while she sings.
- **GURPS:** Force Wall (Line 20 yd, Total Cover), Damage Reflection 8d, Immovable; Regeneration (Fast); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the song stops in the wrong place, as it always does. The wall held.

## Lt X. Patience Haskett — *Thrice*
His tail taps the ground three times, then nine.
- The flail's two heads become **nine**, whirling through a **30-ft radius**.
  - **9d6 bludgeoning** a round (Reflex DC 21 half), and anyone who fails is knocked prone.
- Anyone who strikes him in melee takes a head in return: **3d6**.
- **GURPS:** Damage Aura 9d crushing (Area 10 yd) with Knockdown; Counter Attack 3d; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** a ring of broken stone. Zaheda has started writing down what he counts.

## Lt XI. Rhun Talbridge — *Stand Watch*
He plants the pollaxe butt-down and does not move.
- In a **30-ft radius**, any enemy that moves takes a pollaxe strike: **5d6 + weapon damage**, and its movement ends.
- **Allies within 30 ft** cannot drop below 1 HP for the 3 rounds.
- **GURPS:** Counter Attack (Area 10 yd, ends movement); Unkillable 1 for allies in the area (3 turns); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** a watch relieved by nothing. Ysmay never has to look behind her.

## Lt XII. Edwyn Coldry — *Compline*
He lays the mailed hand flat on the air, the way the brothers laid it on the altar, and sings the night hymn.
- A **30-ft dome** of sung abjuration:
  - No spell or spell-like effect from outside enters it.
  - At the start of each round, every enemy effect inside is targeted by *greater dispel magic* (CL 13).
- His permanent summoned creature sings with him. Its attacks bypass all DR for the duration.
- **GURPS:** Neutralize (Area 10 yd, each turn) with Magic Resistance 10 (Area, Accessibility: from outside); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** silence, then his humming, picking up where the hymn left off.

## Lt XIII. Ashavel Oriym — *Sirocco*
She fans herself once, slowly, and the desert comes through the fan.
- A **60-ft cone** of burning desert wind:
  - **8d6 fire** each round (Reflex DC 21 half).
  - Blinding sand: anyone who fails is blinded for 1 round.
  - Everything in the cone is pushed back 20 ft.
- Ashavel moves through her own wind unseen (total concealment).
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 8d burning (Cone 20 yd) with Knockback and Affliction (Blindness); Obscure (Vision) on herself; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** sand in every seam and the smell of hot stone. Dace's challenger usually never reaches him.

# THE HAND (lesser releases: 3 rounds, DC 21; Chad, 6 Oct 2026)
Chad ruled: the Hand's casting lines stand, and the Hand gets releases. These five are lieutenants of the Menagerie, on the same frame as the eight: bound, never loot, 1/day. **Jin's orders win on Hand operations**, including when to call these releases.

## Lt IV (Hand). Lorne Ashby — *Out of a Small Stone's Shadow*
He hums the three bars of the Moonsea shanty, and the staff splits into its two rods. At Tethford he stepped out of the shadow of a two-foot rock. Now every shadow does that.
- For 3 rounds, **every shadow within 100 ft is a door** for Lorne and for up to **four** of his raised dead.
	- Each round they step out wherever they like.
	- Whatever they strike out of a shadow is flat-footed against that attack and takes **+4d6**.
- Anything slain in the area rises as his servitor at the end of the round (Fort DC 21 for the corpse's spirit to refuse), to a maximum of 4.
- **GURPS:** Warp (Accessibility: shadow to shadow, Area 33 yd, includes 4 Allies); Follow-Up 4d on attacks from shadow; Ally Group (raised, max 4); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the shanty stops on the third bar. The dead he raised go back into the shadows and don't come out.

## Lt V (Hand). Ivrael Quillatar — *The Counted Door*
He counts the exits aloud in Elvish. When he reaches the end, there is only one.
- Within **40 ft**, every exit but one is sealed by force walls. Ivrael stands in the one left open.
- Every enemy within 40 ft must attack him for the 3 rounds (Will DC 21 negates, one save). He gets an attack of opportunity on each one that comes at him.
- **Despair** applies to everything he strikes while it is frightened, and the room is frightened.
- **GURPS:** Force Wall (Area 13 yd, all but one exit), Affliction (Compulsive Attack, Area), Counter Attack against all; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the walls drop and the door is just a door. He counts the exits again, quieter.

## Lt VI (Hand). Durgan Emberlode — *The Banked Coal*
His breath stops smoking. Then the dragon in his blood stops hiding.
- The scale-scarring on his neck becomes scales: **+4 natural armor** and fire immunity for the duration.
- Each round he breathes a **60-ft cone of fire, 10d6** (Reflex DC 21 half).
- Anything within 10 ft of him takes **3d6 fire** at the start of its turn.
- His spells cast in the release count as maximised. Teodric's sword is not the only Overcharge in the Hand.
- **GURPS:** Innate Attack 10d burning (Cone 20 yd), Damage Aura 3d burning (3 yd), DR 4 and Immunity (fire); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the frost doesn't settle on anything near him for a day. The scales go back to scars, a little further up his jaw each time.

## Lt VII (Hand). Nym Esharan — *Eight Vats*
She touches the mask's chin, wets Yashiori, and the Storm King's oldest trick fills the field: the eight-headed serpent was killed drunk.
- **Eight** creatures within 60 ft must save (Will DC 21) or fall into a drunken sleep for the 3 rounds: helpless, and woken only by damage.
- Yashiori's strikes on a sleeper are **automatic critical hits**.
- **The Root Country:** a creature Nym kills during the release **cannot be raised, resurrected or animated for a year and a day**, by anyone, the host's own priests included.
- **GURPS:** Affliction (Sleep, 8 targets, Area 20 yd), Follow-Up crit on sleepers; Cosmic (prevents raising, 1 year and 1 day); Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** the field smells of rice wine. Lorne and Osmund will never get her dead, and the office goes on arguing.

## Lt VIII (Hand). Teodric Halvane — *Old Friends*
He speaks to everyone in the room as if he has known them for years. For three rounds, they believe it.
- Within **40 ft**, every enemy is charmed (Will DC 21; enchanters and the mind-blank warded excepted). They lower their weapons, they don't attack anyone they see Teodric speak to, and **each answers one question truthfully each round**.
- His sword's dispelling stroke runs each round at **CL 16** against one target in the area, at no action.
- Damage breaks the charm only on the creature that took it.
- **GURPS:** Mind Control (Area 13 yd, charm only) with Truthfulness 1/turn; Neutralize (Magic) each turn; Limited Use 1/day.
- **After:** they remember everything they said. Brunna's prisoners talk before she gets to them, which is the only way she has ever lost one.
