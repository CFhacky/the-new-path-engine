# FLAMING BURST — PARTIAL
Source: https://www.d20srd.org/srd/magicItems/magicWeapons.htm (Magic Weapon Special Ability Descriptions) · printed bonus-equivalent / price: +2 bonus, including Flaming's +1 (strong evocation, CL 12th, Craft Magic Arms and Armor and flame blade, flame strike, or fireball)
Source text (key facts, quoted briefly): "functions as a flaming weapon that also explodes with flame upon striking a successful critical hit. The fire does not harm the wielder"; "an extra 1d10 points of fire damage on a successful critical hit. If the weapon's critical multiplier is x3, add an extra 2d10 ... if the multiplier is x4, add an extra 3d10"; "Even if the flaming ability is not active, the weapon still deals its extra fire damage on a successful critical hit." SRD generic rules: additional dice are not multiplied on a crit; a crit-triggered special effect also works against creatures immune to crits (roll the confirm, apply the effect).
Existing pool coverage: Flaming (Elemental 01-08, T4 = 1d6 fire; IA Burning, Follow-Up) covers the base. Lethal Focus (Offensive 31-36) is untyped crit-only dice; Elemental Burst (Elemental 75-80) is an area burst. Gap: neither is the printed fire rider on a confirmed crit scaled by critical multiplier.
Verdict: PARTIAL. Flaming already supplies the base; emit only the crit-fire rider. One printed rung (+2 total); no tiers invented.

Translation identity: flat (dice are fixed by the weapon's multiplier class; wielder Str, enhancement and the crit multiplier never scale them) · armor-agnostic (the crit must land; fire resistance applies, DR does not apply to energy) · no cap, no stacking, no proc d100 (the confirmed crit is the trigger).
3.5e (delta): On a confirmed critical hit, add fire damage: x2 weapon 1d10, x3 weapon 2d10, x4 weapon 3d10 (x5+ not printed; fork 3). Not multiplied by the crit. Works with the flaming toggle off, and against creatures immune to crits. Fire immunity/resistance applies. Ammunition inherits it (bow, crossbow, sling). No save, no SR, no DC. No healing interaction.
GURPS (delta): Chassis Gadget (Breakable DR 6 -25%, Can Be Stolen -10% = -35%). Second Innate Attack (Burning), Follow-Up (+0%), Trigger: critical hit only, 1d / 2d / 3d by the weapon's multiplier class (Lethal Focus precedent maps 1d6/2d6/3d6 to 1d/2d/3d; the d10 vs d6 difference is not carried, fork 2). Trigger percentage is "Variable" in the index, so OPEN. Magical -10%. Base Innate Attack cost per die also open; no point total.
Pricing: Printed +2 total = 8,000 gp (bonus-squared x 2,000), sold as one affix that replaces Flaming (not Flaming 2,000 + a separate +1 delta; fork 1).
Name collision: pool Elemental Burst (Elemental 75-80, 10-ft area, Ref half; NOT this ability, since the SRD burst is not an area); pool Flaming; SRD Icy Burst, Shocking Burst, Thundering (queued; same crit-burst template).
Forks needing a ruling:
1. Price when combined. Recommended default: Flaming Burst is a standalone +2 that includes Flaming; never stack Flaming and Flaming Burst on one weapon.
2. GURPS dice mapping for d10. Recommended default: 1d / 2d / 3d, as Lethal Focus; revisit if d10 average (5.5) needs a +1.
3. GURPS has no critical multiplier. Recommended default: use the weapon's 3.5e multiplier class (x2/x3/x4) to pick 1d/2d/3d; x5+ not printed, so no rung.
