The DMG OCR route was permission-denied; not read. · printed bonus-equivalent: +5 (no flat gp printed). CL 18, strong necromancy; slashing weapons only.
Source text (key facts, quoted briefly): "allows the weapon to sever the heads of those it strikes. Upon a roll of natural 20 (followed by a successful roll to confirm the critical hit), the weapon severs the opponent's head (if it has one)... Some creatures, such as many aberrations and all oozes, have no heads. Others, such as golems and undead creatures other than vampires, are not affected by the loss of their heads. Most other creatures, however, die when their heads are cut off. The DM may have to make judgement calls... A vorpal weapon must be slashing weapon."
Existing pool coverage: none that matches. "Vorpal Edge" (Offensive 67-72) shares the name and the natural-20 trigger but is bonus slashing damage (base die x1..x3), not decapitation. Fortification (Defensive 07-12) negates crits and so blocks this. Gap: the instant-kill effect.
Verdict: new family, one rung (+5), SRD-faithful; do not merge with Vorpal Edge.
Translation identity: flat, no scaling · armor interaction: none; DR is irrelevant once the crit confirms · cap behavior: state change, not a bigger number: head severed or no effect.
3.5e: Natural 20, then a successful confirmation roll. The head is severed with no save and no SR. No effect on headless creatures (many aberrations, all oozes) or those unaffected by head loss (golems, undead other than vampires); creatures immune to crits cannot be vorpaled. Slashing weapons only. No d100 (printed trigger is the confirmed 20). The text does not call it a death effect, so Death Ward does not stop it. No Fatal Wound interaction. Fused-engine resolution: a confirmed natural-20 crit forces Head/Lethal on the location table.
GURPS: no instant-kill trait verified in my sources. Mechanism: trigger on a GURPS critical hit on the attack roll, effect "head severed", applying the same headless/unaffected exceptions. The 3.5e trigger is about 5% x confirm chance, while GURPS critical hits are 3-4 (and 5-6 at high skill), so the rates differ; the book rules and any trigger limitation are not retrieved and are open. Gadget -35%. Points: not computable.
Pricing: +5 = 50,000 gp.
Name collision: Vorpal Edge (Offensive 67-72), SRD vorpal sword (item), Keen Edge row (the SRD prerequisite spell is keen edge).
Rulings (final): 1. A confirmed natural-20 crit from a vorpal weapon forces the Head location at Lethal severity on the fused engine's crit table; there is no separate save and the same table applies to PCs and named characters. 2. Not a death effect. 3. GURPS uses the same engine table.
