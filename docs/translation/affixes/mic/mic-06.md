# MIC weapon properties, batch 06 (Hunting to Impaling)
> **Fused-engine resolution applies:** where this draft's GURPS dice, DR handling, negative-level stand-in, GURPS alignment tag or name-collision rename differ from `docs/translation/FUSED_ENGINE_RESOLUTION.md`, that file wins (dice read as-is; riders ignore worn DR; Energy Drained condition; alignment stays on the 3.5e side; names stay, never merge).

## HUNTING (printed +1; moderate abjuration, CL 6th; prereq greater magic fang) — NEW
Source: increases your damage bonus by 4 against your favored enemies (ranger class feature).
Pool: Hunter's Mark (Offensive 55-60) is a marked-target bonus, not a favored-enemy bonus. 3.5e: +4 damage vs favored enemies, a flat bonus that requires the ranger feature (class-gated); not multiplied on a crit. GURPS: +2 damage vs favored enemies (2:1 default), requires the campaign's ranger analogue (OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. GURPS conversion of a flat +4 (default +2).

## IGNAN (printed +2; moderate evocation, CL 7th; prereq fire subtype) — PARTIAL
Opposed-subtype weapon keyed to the water subtype; identical to Aquan (batch 01): DR bypass, extra 2d6 vs such targets, negative level on a water-subtype wielder. Same delta over Banefire, price (+2 = 8,000 gp) and forks.

## ILLUMINATING (printed +500 gp; faint evocation, CL 5th; prereq light) — NEW
Source: when drawn, glows with bright light in a 20-foot radius and shadowy light for another 20 feet.
Pool: none. 3.5e/GURPS: as printed (GURPS Light-source, no combat effect). Price: printed 500 gp flat. Forks: none.

## ILLUSION BANE (printed +1; moderate divination, CL 10th; prereq true seeing, dispel magic) — NEW
Source: attacks ignore any miss chance created by an illusion effect (including effects that mimic illusions, such as displacement), though you must still target the correct square against total concealment; continuous. Once per day (swift command) destroy illusions in one of two forms: after hitting a creature, make a dispel check (1d20+10) against each illusion spell on the target, as a targeted dispel magic limited to the illusion school, with a separate check for each; or touch a single illusion with the weapon and speak the command to dispel it (a successful check against any part dispels the whole effect).
Pool: Dispelling Strike (Condition 85-90) is school-agnostic and 1/encounter; DMG Seeking is the miss-chance half for ranged weapons. 3.5e: as printed; fixed +10 on the dispel check (flat-source), 1/day. GURPS: ignore illusion-based attack penalties plus an Neutralize (Illusion) once per day (modifier OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## ILLUSION THEFT (SYNERGY, printed +2; strong divination, CL 17th; prereq Illusion Bane) — NEW (builds on Illusion Bane)
Source: functions as illusion bane; the first illusion spell the weapon dispels is stored in it (spell storing rules, but any level, only a spell it dispelled, no need to strike a creature to activate); the stored spell keeps its original effect with its duration paused at theft; you learn its effect and remaining duration, may activate it at any time on a new target, and the duration resumes then; once discharged the property is unusable until another illusion is dispelled and stored.
Pool: none (Reservoir stores a spell cast into it; this steals one). 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: a stolen-effect container that holds one dispelled effect (cost and the duration-pause mechanism OPEN). Price: printed +2 = 8,000 gp on top of Illusion Bane. Forks: 1. which illusions of a PC or named NPC can be taken (default as printed; the weapon's own CL check against the spell).

## IMPACT (printed +1; moderate evocation, CL 10th; bludgeoning weapon; prereq weapon of impact) — COVERED
Source: doubles the threat range of a bludgeoning weapon (a quarterstaff 19-20, a heavy flail 17-20); does not stack with other threat-range effects.
Pool: Keen Edge (Offensive 13-18), the same effect for any weapon at x1.5 (see DMG Keen for the ratified x1.5 versus printed doubling).
Verdict: COVERED by Keen Edge. Fork: the pool row has no weapon-type restriction, so Keen (piercing and slashing) and Impact (bludgeoning) are one affix; default one row for all weapon types. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp.

## IMPALING (printed +1; moderate transmutation, CL 9th; piercing melee; prereq find the gap) — NEW
Source: swift (command), three times per day: the next attack before end of turn is resolved as a touch attack (declare before the attack roll; a miss wastes the use).
Pool: Armor Piercing (Offensive 19-24) ignores DR, not AC; Precise Thrust (temper 23A-4) is +2/+3/+4 against armor. 3.5e: as printed, 3/day. GURPS: the attack ignores worn and natural armor DR (Armor Divisor "ignores", OPEN) for one attack, Limited Use 3/day. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. the pool's Armor Divisor ladder tops at 5, so "ignore all armor" needs an open step.
