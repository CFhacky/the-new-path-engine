## SOULBOUND (printed +1 item level; moderate abjuration, CL 6th; prereq magic weapon, essentia pool 2) — NEW (incarnum) [INACTIVE until a psionic/incarnum character exists]
Source: serves as a receptacle for essentia; each essentia point invested raises the enhancement bonus on attack and damage by 1, to a maximum +3; capacity is the lower of 2 and your normal essentia capacity; chakra binds: arms, +2 insight on rolls to confirm crits; brow, reroll one concealment miss chance roll once per round; hands, +2 insight on initiative while held. Swift (mental) activation.
Pool: Striking (Offensive 01-06) is a fixed enhancement ladder; this scales with an invested resource, stated explicitly. 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Weapon Bond +1..+3 by invested essentia and chakra bonuses as skill bonuses (essentia-to-ER exchange OPEN). Price: printed +1 bonus = 2,000 gp (the maximum +3 is a conditional bonus; flagged). Forks: 1. repricing (default price at the printed +1).

## SOULBREAKER (SYNERGY, printed +1; strong necromancy, CL 17th; prereq Enervating, energy drain) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as an enervating weapon, but its negative levels do not fade after 1 hour: after a delay the OCR renders as "24h" (OCR-uncertain), if the levels have not been purged the subject must make a DC 18 Fortitude save for each negative level or lose a character level.
Pool: builds on Enervating (batch 04). Delta = the delayed permanent-loss save. 3.5e: as printed; permanent level loss is campaign-weighty, so the default is to apply it only to NPCs unless the owner rules otherwise. GURPS: lasting loss of points or a skill level on a failed HT roll (OPEN). Price: +1 on top of Enervating. RULING (final): NPC wielders use it as printed; against a PC the negative levels last 24 hours unless purged and never convert to level loss. The 24-hour delay is OCR-uncertain; confirm against the book page.

## SOULDRINKING (SYNERGY, printed +1; faint necromancy, CL 5th; melee; prereq Enervating, vampiric touch) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as an enervating weapon; on a critical hit against a living creature it grants you 5 temporary hit points and a +2 morale bonus on melee damage rolls for 10 minutes; the temporary hit points do not stack with other sources.
Pool: Enervating (batch 04); Body Feeder (batch 02) is the nearest temp-HP-on-crit. Delta = fixed 5 temp HP and +2 morale damage. 3.5e: as printed; flat. GURPS: temporary HP buffer of 5, +1 damage for 10 minutes (2:1 default, OPEN). Price: +1 on top of Enervating. Forks: none.

## STUNNING (SYNERGY, printed +1; moderate enchantment, CL 9th; prereq Screaming, hold monster) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as a screaming weapon; on a successful critical hit the target must succeed on a DC 17 Fortitude save or be stunned for 1 round; ammunition.
Pool: Dazing (Condition 17-24) is a 1/encounter Will daze; Screaming (batch 10) is the base. Delta = crit-triggered stun, fixed DC 17. GURPS: Affliction (Stunned), Trigger critical, HT roll (percentages OPEN). Price: +1 on top of Screaming. Forks: none.

## STUNNING SURGE (printed +1; moderate enchantment, CL 9th; prereq hold monster) — NEW
Source: swift (command) on a successful melee attack: a surge of magical energy; unless the target succeeds on a Fortitude save (DC 10 + one-half your character level + your Charisma modifier) it is stunned for 1 round; uses per day = 1 + your Charisma bonus; attunement.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed; the DC and the uses scale with the wielder (stated; not flat-source). GURPS: Affliction (Stunned) with an HT roll penalized by the wielder's level analogue, Limited Use (1 + Will-based Cha stand-in, OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## STYGIAN (printed +1; moderate necromancy, CL 9th; prereq enervation) — NEW
Source: swift (mental), three times per day: the next successful attack before end of turn bestows one negative level on the target, lasting 10 minutes (so it cannot become a permanent level decrease), in addition to normal damage.
Pool: none (Enervating is crit-triggered; this is activated, no crit needed, no save printed). 3.5e: as printed, 3/day. The negative level is the Energy Drained condition for 10 minutes; usable 3/day. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. no save printed (default as printed, limited by 3/day).

## SUNDERING (printed +1; faint transmutation, CL 5th; melee; prereq Improved Sunder, weapon of impact or metaphysical weapon) — NEW

## SWEEPING (printed +1; faint transmutation, CL 5th; melee; prereq bull's strength) — NEW

Pool: none (Titan's Grip's staff reading gives trip, disarm and sunder bonuses on an oversize weapon). 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: +2 on the Trip contest (OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.
