# MIC weapon properties, batch 02 (Bloodfeeding to Collision)
Source: Magic Item Compendium pp. 30-32, read from D:\Backup\I-drive\Sourcebooks\_text (OCR). Drafts, not ratified. Conventions as in mic-01 (Gadget -35%, point totals OPEN, bonus-squared pricing, ammunition as printed).

## BLOODFEEDING (printed +1; moderate necromancy, CL 7th; melee; prereq vampiric touch) — NEW
Source: each time the weapon damages a living creature it gains 1 blood point, stored up to 1 hour, maximum 10; when you deal damage you can spend up to 5 stored points, each dealing an extra 2 damage; no point is gained on the strike where you spend.
Pool: none (Leech and Bloodprice are different). It is an accumulator, so the stacking doctrine applies: additive, linear.
3.5e: as printed. Caps stated: 10 stored, 5 spent per strike (+10 damage maximum), 1 hour storage. Flat-source: the +2 per point never scales. Bloodless targets (constructs, undead, oozes, elementals) grant no points. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up, +2 damage per point spent (1 point = +2 in GURPS damage, defaults to +1 per point at the 2:1 conversion, OPEN), Trigger "spend stored points". Price: +1 = 2,000 gp.
Forks: 1. conversion of +2 per point to GURPS (default +1 per point). 2. points decay after 1 hour (as printed).

## BLOODSTONE (printed +1; moderate necromancy, CL 10th; melee; prereq Empower Spell, vampiric touch) — PARTIAL
Source: can store and cast one vampiric touch against a creature it strikes, as a spell storing weapon, automatically empowered; stores only that spell, one at a time; free (command) activation.
Pool: Reservoir (Resource 49-54) stores a spell; DMG Spell Storing (see that file) is the delivery pattern. Delta = fixed spell, empowered.
3.5e: as printed, CL of the weapon (10th). GURPS: stored attack, Follow-Up, the stored spell's damage empowered x1.5 (rounding OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. shares the Spell Storing rulings.

## BLURSTRIKE (printed +2; moderate illusion, CL 6th; melee; prereq blur) — NEW
Source: swift (command) activation; the weapon fades for 1 round; the opponent is flat-footed against the first attack you make with it that round; creatures that do not rely on sight (blindsight) and creatures with uncanny dodge are not flat-footed against it; ten times per day.
Pool: none.
3.5e: as printed, 10 uses/day, no save. GURPS: the target is Surprised against the first attack of the round (the combat modifier is OPEN); sight-independent defenders and Danger Sense are excluded. Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: none.

## BODY FEEDER (printed +3; moderate necromancy, CL 9th; prereq vampiric touch or claws of the vampire) — NEW
Source: on a critical hit against a living creature, gain temporary HP equal to half the damage dealt by the critical hit; lasts up to 1 minute; no stacking with any other source, including further crits with this weapon.
Pool: none (Leech is flat HP per hit; Life Shield is a fixed temp-HP block). It scales with the crit's damage, so it is NOT flat-source; stated explicitly.
3.5e: as printed. Bloodless targets give nothing. GURPS: temporary HP buffer equal to half the injury dealt, 1 minute (mechanism; a "Temporary HP" trait and cost OPEN). Price: +3 = 18,000 gp. Forks: 1. cap per crit (default none, as printed; the no-stack rule is the brake).

## BRASH (printed +1; faint abjuration, CL 5th; melee; prereq remove fear) — NEW
Source: if you enter a rage wielding it, the rage lasts 3 rounds longer; while raging with it you are immune to fear effects.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed (class-gated by rage). GURPS: extend the campaign rage by 3 turns (3 seconds each; exact extension OPEN) and Fearlessness while raging. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: GURPS rage stand-in as for Berserker.

## BRUTAL SURGE (printed +1; moderate evocation, CL 11th; melee; prereq Bigby's forceful hand) — PARTIAL
Source: swift (command) after a successful melee attack: a bull rush against the target, no attack of opportunity, resolved with your size and Strength, +2 on the check if wielded in two hands; pushes the target back the greatest possible distance, you do not move; uses per day = 1 + Con bonus; once activated it cannot be activated by another creature until the next day.
Pool: Knockback (Condition 49-54) pushes 5-20 ft on a Fortitude save; this uses an opposed Strength check and a no-move push. Delta = the opposed-check bull rush and the daily-use rule.
3.5e: as printed; this is a scaling effect by wielder Str and Con, stated explicitly, not flat-source. GURPS: Knockback-style push resolved by a Quick Contest of ST; uses per day 1 + HT bonus (stand-in for Con bonus, OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. the attunement rule ("cannot be activated by another creature until the following day"), default keep as printed.

## CHANGELING (printed +2,000 gp; faint transmutation, CL 5th; spear, shortspear or longspear; prereq shrink item) — PARTIAL
Source: swift (command) once per round: change between spear, shortspear and longspear sized for you; at the same time choose any wood, stone, metal or combination appearance and decoration; the real composition does not change.
Pool: Chameleon (Utility 91-96) covers appearance change. Delta = the resize among the three spear types.
3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Morph (cosmetic) already used by the pool row, plus switching reach and damage profile among three weapon types; cost OPEN. Price: printed 2,000 gp flat. Forks: none.

## CHARGEBREAKER (printed +1; faint enchantment, CL 5th; melee; prereq daze monster) — NEW
Source: a charging creature hit by this weapon must succeed on a DC 14 Fortitude save or be knocked prone.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed; fixed DC 14 (flat-source), no daily limit, passive. GURPS: a HT roll or the target falls down (Knockdown-style effect; the modifier OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. does it need damage past DR to trigger (default yes, a hit that deals damage).

## CHARGING (printed +1; moderate transmutation, CL 5th; melee; prereq Spirited Charge, keen edge) — COVERED
Source: while mounted, an extra 2d6 damage on a successful charge attack.
Pool: Devastating Charge (Offensive 91-96): +1d6/+1d6/+2d6/+3d6/+4d6 on a charge; GURPS Slam Attack extra dice with charge-only Accessibility. The printed 2d6 equals the T3 value, and the printed "mounted" condition is an extra restriction that makes it weaker, not stronger.
Verdict: COVERED at T3; carry "mounted only" on the item card. Price: printed +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## COLLISION (printed +2; moderate transmutation, CL 6th; prereq weapon of impact) — NEW
Source: an extra 5 points of damage with each hit; ammunition.
Pool: Wounding (Offensive 07-12, bonus damage +1/+1d4/+1d6/+2d6/+3d6) is dice-based and tiered; this is a flat +5.
3.5e: +5 untyped damage per hit, not multiplied on a crit. GURPS: +2 to damage per hit (the 2:1 conversion rounded, OPEN) or Innate Attack Follow-Up 1d+... (OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. GURPS conversion of a flat +5 (default +2).
