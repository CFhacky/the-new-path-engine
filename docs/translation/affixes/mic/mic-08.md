## MINDCRUSHER (printed +2; strong necromancy, CL 12th; prereq vampiric touch or psychic vampire) — NEW
Source: a psionic creature struck loses power points equal to half the hit point damage dealt (base weapon damage only; Strength and extras do not count); a creature with no power points, including nonpsionic creatures, must make a DC 17 Will save or take 1 point of Wisdom damage; ammunition.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed, fixed DC 17. GURPS: ER or FP drain on psionic targets and a Will roll against 1 point of IQ-equivalent damage (OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. Wisdom damage to nonpsionic creatures on every hit (default as printed).

## MINDFEEDER (printed +1; strong necromancy, CL 15th; prereq vampiric touch or psychic vampire) — NEW
Source: on a critical hit gain 1 temporary power point per 5 damage dealt by the crit, lasting up to 1 minute, gained even if the target has none; temporary points overlap rather than stack (higher value wins); crit-immune creatures do not trigger it; ammunition.
Pool: none; the parallel of Body Feeder (batch 02) for power points. It scales with crit damage, not flat-source (stated). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## MORPHING (printed +1; moderate transmutation, CL 9th; melee or thrown; prereq fabricate) — NEW
Source: standard activation: reshape into any other melee or thrown weapon of the same size and type (light, one-handed, two-handed); double weapons keep the magical bonus on one end only and the other end is masterwork; a double weapon turned single can take the properties of either end, the other end's properties dormant, not lost.
Pool: Changeling (batch 02) is spear-only; Chameleon changes appearance. 3.5e/GURPS: as printed; GURPS reshapes into any weapon of the same weight class and reach profile (modifier OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## NECROTIC FOCUS (printed +3; moderate necromancy, CL 7th; melee; prereq enervation, spectral hand) — NEW
Source: serves as a channel for your ability drain or energy drain supernatural ability: while wielding it you deal ability drain or bestow negative levels through it as with natural weapons; if a save is allowed, add the weapon's enhancement bonus to the DC.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed, gated by a monster or class drain ability. GURPS: deliver the drain through the weapon (OPEN). Price: +3 = 18,000 gp. Forks: none.

## PARALYTIC BURST (printed +2; moderate enchantment, CL 9th; prereq hold monster) — NEW
Source: on a critical hit a wave of green energy paralyzes the target for 1 round (Will DC 17 negates); applies even to creatures that take no extra damage from crits.
Pool: Dazing (Condition 17-24) is a 1/encounter daze on a Will save; this is crit-triggered paralysis. 3.5e: as printed, fixed DC 17. GURPS: Affliction (Paralysis), Trigger critical, HT or Will resistance (percentages OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. immunity: paralysis-immune creatures are unaffected.

## PARALYZING (printed +1; moderate enchantment, CL 10th; melee; prereq hold monster) — NEW
Source: swift activation, once per day: the next creature struck must make a DC 17 Will save or be paralyzed; each round on its turn it can make a new save to end the effect; otherwise it lasts 10 rounds.
Pool: Dazing is a daze, not paralysis. 3.5e: as printed, fixed DC 17, 1/day. GURPS: Affliction (Paralysis) with a Will roll each second to end it (10-turn cap), Limited Use 1/day (percentages OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## PARRYING (printed +2; strong enchantment, CL 15th; prereq divine protection or defensive precognition) — PARTIAL (composite)
Source: +1 insight bonus to AC and +1 insight bonus on saves whenever you hold it, even when flat-footed.
Pool: Warding (Defensive 01-06, AC) and Stalwart (13-18, all saves) at T5-T4 supply +1 each. Delta = the insight bonus type, which stacks with their deflection and resistance bonuses.
3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Defense Bonus +1 and +1 to resist (Will and HT) while held (Gadget). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: none.

## POWER STORING (printed +1; strong evocation, CL 12th; prereq manifester level 12th) — PARTIAL
Source: store a single targeted power of up to 5 power points (manifesting time 1 standard action); when the weapon strikes and damages a creature it can manifest the power on it; once manifested the weapon is empty and can be refilled; a randomly generated one has a 50% chance to hold a power; the weapon whispers the stored power's name.
Pool: DMG Spell Storing and Reservoir (Resource 49-54). Delta = psionic variant. Same rulings as Spell Storing. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: gated by psionics.

## PRECISE (printed +1; faint evocation, CL 5th; ranged; prereq Precise Shot) — NEW
Source: shoot or throw at an opponent engaged in melee without the standard -4 penalty; does not apply if you already have Precise Shot.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: no penalty for shooting into melee (the penalty for a melee-engaged target; OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## PRISMATIC BURST (printed +30,000 gp; strong evocation, CL 13th; prereq prismatic spray) — NEW (high risk)
Source: on any critical hit, multicolored light subjects the target to a prismatic spray (save DC 20, PH 264); applies even to targets that take no extra damage from crits.
Pool: none. The spell's seven rays include instant-death and petrification outcomes; this is the strongest crit rider in the batch.
3.5e: as printed, fixed DC 20. GURPS: a randomized effect from a seven-entry table (effects OPEN). Price: printed 30,000 gp flat. Forks: 1. use against PCs and named bosses (default: the effect works as printed on PCs; a named boss is a ruling at ratification). 2. whether the effect should be capped to the weaker rays for a rolled (non-authored) drop (default no, authored Unique-only).
