# MIC weapon properties, batch 05 (Ethereal Reaver to Holy Surge)
Source: Magic Item Compendium pp. 35-37, read from D:\Backup\I-drive\Sourcebooks\_text (OCR). Drafts, not ratified. Conventions as in mic-01.

## ETHEREAL REAVER (printed +3; strong divination, CL 12th; melee; prereq see invisibility) — PARTIAL (composite)
Source: functions as a ghost touch weapon; also lets you see invisible creatures as under see invisibility.
Pool: DMG Ghost Touch (see that file) covers the first half; Truesight (Utility 31-36) gives See Invisibility 1/day at T4, at will only at T1.
3.5e: Ghost Touch plus continuous see invisibility while wielding. GURPS: Ghost Touch's Affects Insubstantial plus See Invisible (Gadget; costs OPEN). Price: printed +3 = 18,000 gp (a +1 base ability plus a continuous detection effect). Forks: none beyond Ghost Touch's.

## EVERBRIGHT (printed +2,000 gp; strong evocation, CL 12th; prereq searing light) — NEW
Source: standard (command), twice per day, flash a brilliant light: all creatures within 20 feet of you are blinded for 1 round (Reflex DC 14 negates); the weapon is immune to acid and rust.
Pool: Blinding (Condition 43-48) is crit-triggered single-target; this is a self-centered burst. 3.5e: as printed, fixed DC 14, 2/day. GURPS: Affliction (Blindness) with an Area effect centered on the wielder (Emanation -20%), DX or reaction roll to avoid, Limited Use 2/day (percentages OPEN). Price: printed 2,000 gp flat. Forks: 1. does it blind the wielder and allies within 20 ft (printed "all creatures within 20 feet of you"); default yes, the wielder included unless blind-immune.

## FIERCEBANE (SYNERGY, printed +1; strong conjuration, CL 12th; prereq Bane, summon monster I) — PARTIAL
Source: acts as a bane weapon against the type or subtype it was attuned to; glows when a designated foe comes within 60 feet, even unseen; extra damage on every successful critical hit: x2 1d10, x3 2d10, x4 3d10; ammunition.
Pool: DMG Bane (see that file) plus the burst template. Delta = crit rider and 60-ft detection. 3.5e/GURPS: crit rider by multiplier class (default 1d / 2d / 3d), detection 60 ft as a proximity sense. Price: +1 on top of Bane. Forks: shares the Bane rulings (foe chosen by the crafter).

## FLESHGRINDING (printed +2; moderate transmutation, CL 11th; piercing or slashing melee; prereq animate objects) — NEW
Source: free (command) when you deal damage to a living creature in melee: you let go and the weapon animates, grinding itself into the foe; at the start of your turn each round for 5 rounds it automatically damages that creature as a normal hit (enhancement bonus, other weapon properties and your Strength bonus included, feats like Power Attack excluded); a standard action pulls it free (anyone but you needs a DC 20 Strength check); afterward it returns to your hand as returning, unless the target holds it.
Pool: none (Fatal Wound is a stacking bleed with a proc; this is a weapon-driven repeating hit). 3.5e: as printed. It scales with the wielder's Strength and the weapon's properties, stated explicitly; no daily limit printed. Bloodless targets: the text says "living creature", so constructs and undead are immune. GURPS: repeated Innate Attack equal to a normal hit each second for 5 turns, ended by pulling it free (Contest of ST DC-equivalent, OPEN); Cyclic is the cost carrier (+400% per cycle, from the Registry constants) but the percentage and the Follow-Up interplay are OPEN. Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. the wielder is unarmed for 5 rounds; default as printed.

## FORCE (printed +2; moderate evocation, CL 9th; projectile; prereq magic missile) — NEW
Source: ammunition becomes a force attack: automatically overcomes damage reduction, no miss chance against incorporeal targets, no damage to creatures immune to force; same damage as normal ammunition.
Pool: Armor Piercing (Offensive 19-24: ignore DR 2/3/5/8/10) is capped; this bypasses all DR. 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Armor Divisor "ignores DR" (the pool's Armor Divisor ladder tops at 5; the "ignore" step is OPEN) plus Affects Insubstantial. Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. force-immune targets (only the printed ones).

## GHOST STRIKE (SYNERGY, printed +1; moderate conjuration, CL 11th; melee; prereq Ghost Touch, undeath to death) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as ghost touch; sneak attacks and critical hits against undead affect them as if they were living creatures.
Pool: DMG Ghost Touch (see that file). Delta = precision and crit immunity of undead removed. 3.5e/GURPS: as printed; in GURPS the weapon's attacks ignore the Unliving crit and precision immunities (named trait handling OPEN). Price: +1 on top of Ghost Touch. Forks: none.

## HARMONIZING (printed +1; faint illusion, CL 5th; melee; prereq ghost sound, bardic music) — NEW
Source: +2 competence bonus on Perform (sing); if you hold it when you begin a bardic music effect, one round later the weapon continues the performance for 10 rounds, until you begin another effect, or until commanded to stop (swift mental).
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed; class-gated by bardic music. GURPS: +2 to Singing and a Delayed ongoing Bard Song effect for 10 turns (campaign bard analogue named at ratification, OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## HEAVENLY BURST (printed +1; moderate evocation, CL 7th; prereq holy smite) — NEW
Source: on a critical hit against an evil creature, a shower of radiance deals 3d6 damage and blinds it for 1 round; a successful DC 14 Fortitude save negates the blindness; ammunition.
Pool: Radiant (Elemental 45-50) is always-on positive energy damage. This is crit-triggered and evil-keyed. 3.5e: as printed, fixed DC 14, damage is not multiplied. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up 2d (Executioner T3 for 3d6 is a proxy, OPEN), Trigger critical, Accessibility evil only (percentage OPEN), blindness Affliction. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: shared alignment-tag ruling (GURPS has no alignment axis, see DMG Holy).

## HIDEAWAY (printed +2,000 gp; faint transmutation, CL 5th; prereq shrink item) — NEW
Source: swift (command): folds into a bundle two size categories smaller than you; +2 Sleight of Hand to conceal it as if it were a dagger; a second command (swift) unfolds it.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Gadget with Shrinking-style concealment and +2 to Concealment-type skill (Sleight of Hand analogue; costs OPEN). Price: printed 2,000 gp flat. Forks: none.

## HOLY SURGE (SYNERGY, printed +1; moderate evocation, CL 11th; melee; prereq Holy, holy smite or holy word) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as a holy weapon (continuous); on a successful melee attack, command a burst: against an evil target extra 3d6, against a non-evil target nothing and the use is wasted; uses per day = 1 + Cha bonus; attunement.
Pool: DMG Holy (see that file) plus the Energy Surge on-demand burst pattern. 3.5e: as printed; scales with Cha bonus for uses (stated). GURPS: Holy's Follow-Up plus a 3d6 burst, Limited Use (1 + Will-based Cha stand-in, OPEN). Price: +1 on top of Holy. Forks: shared alignment-tag ruling.
