Source: standard (command) toggle; sheathed in negative energy, harmless to the wielder, until a second command; extra 1d8 damage to any living creature on a hit; ammunition.
Pool: Shadowtouch (Elemental 39-44): +1d4/+1d6/+1d8/+2d6/+3d6 negative energy, undead take half. The printed 1d8 equals the T3 value.
Verdict: COVERED at T3; carry the "living creatures only" and command-word toggle on the card. Price: printed +2 = 8,000 gp (the MIC prices it above a +1 elemental die, noted for the Registry owner). Forks: 1. price mismatch against other elemental dice (default: printed +2 stands for this named property).

## CURSESPEWING (printed +3; moderate necromancy, CL 11th; prereq bestow curse) — NEW
Source: on any critical hit, bestows a curse: -4 on attack rolls, saves, skill checks and ability checks for 1 minute; multiple strikes do not stack; no save printed.
Pool: Weakening and Mind Fog (Condition pool) are narrower and use saves. 3.5e: as printed, trigger = confirmed critical hit, fixed -4, no save, no SR beyond the weapon's own (the printed text allows none; flagged). GURPS: Affliction-style curse, -4 to all success rolls for 1 minute, Trigger critical (percentage OPEN). Price: +3 = 18,000 gp. Forks: 1. the printed property allows no save; default keep as printed, since it is +3 and crit-only.

Pool: Sneak's Edge (Skill/Class 13-18): +1d6/+1d6/+2d6/+2d6/+3d6 sneak attack damage. Verdict: COVERED at T5-T4. Price +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## DEFENSIVE SURGE (printed +1; faint abjuration, CL 5th; melee; prereq shield) — NEW
Source: swift (command) after a successful melee attack in a round in which you use Combat Expertise or fight defensively: an additional +2 AC until your next turn; uses per day = 1 + Int bonus; once activated cannot be activated by another creature until the next day.
Pool: nearest Warding (Defensive 01-06, always-on deflection AC) and Aegis; neither is conditional or limited-use.
3.5e: as printed. This scales with Int bonus for daily uses (stated). GURPS: +1 to Defense Bonus (+2 AC at roughly 1 DB) until the wielder's next turn, trigger "after a hit while on All-Out/Defensive attack posture" (OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. GURPS stand-in for Combat Expertise (default Defensive Attack).

## DESICCATING (printed +1; moderate necromancy, CL 5th; prereq desiccating bubble) — NEW
Source: destroys water in a living creature; extra 1d4 damage (1d8 against plants and water-subtype elementals); ammunition.
Pool: none (Venomous is poison; Shadowtouch is negative energy). 3.5e: +1d4 untyped on a hit (+1d8 vs plants and water elementals), not multiplied on a crit; bloodless targets that have no water (constructs, undead) take none. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up 1d-2 (Wounding ladder precedent 1d4 = 1d-2; 1d8 = 1d+1 for the vulnerable targets, Hunter's Mark precedent). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## DESICCATING BURST (SYNERGY, printed +1; strong necromancy, CL 12th; prereq Desiccating, horrid wilting) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as Desiccating; on a critical hit extra damage by multiplier x2 1d8, x3 2d8, x4 3d8, doubled against plants and water-subtype elementals (applies even to creatures immune to crit extra damage); the struck creature is fatigued for 8 hours or until it drinks a gallon of water; ammunition.
Pool: none for the burst; Desiccating (above) is the base. Delta = crit rider plus fatigue.
3.5e: as printed; fatigue has no save printed (flagged). GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up, Trigger critical, 1d / 2d / 3d (default) doubled vs plants and water elementals, plus the Fatigue effect (FP loss, OPEN). Price: +1 over Desiccating. Forks: 1. the printed fatigue allows no save.

## DISARMING (printed +2; moderate transmutation, CL 5th; prereq bull's strength) — NEW
Source: +2 bonus on your disarm attempts; opponents cannot disarm you of this weapon.
Pool: none. 3.5e: as printed (a +2 competence-style bonus; immune to disarm for the weapon). GURPS: +2 on the contest to disarm (Weapon Bond-style bonus) and the weapon cannot be taken by a Disarm technique (Weapon Bond precedent, cost OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: none.

## DISLOCATOR (printed +1; strong conjuration, CL 12th; prereq teleport) and DISLOCATOR, GREAT (SYNERGY, printed +1; prereq Dislocator, greater teleport) — NEW (two rungs)
Source: swift (mental) activation; the next successful attack before end of turn teleports the target up to 10 ft in a direction you choose, Will DC 17 negates; cannot teleport into an occupied space (the attempt is wasted); three times per day; ammunition. Great: up to 30 ft, Will DC 20, same limits.
Pool: Knockback (Condition 49-54) pushes; this teleports. Phasewalk is the wielder's own teleport.

GURPS: Teleport the target (Affliction-style with a Will resistance roll, 3/day; cost OPEN). Price: +1 and +1 (the Great adds +1 on top, printed). Forks: 1. does the 10-ft and 30-ft teleport provoke or carry the target into hazards (default yes, wielder's choice of destination, no safe-landing guarantee).

## DISPELLING (printed +1; faint abjuration, CL 5th; prereq dispel magic) — COVERED

Pool: Dispelling Strike (Condition 85-90): on hit 1/encounter, targeted dispel at CL 3/5/8/12/16; GURPS Neutralize (Magic). The printed CL 5th matches the T4 rung.
Verdict: COVERED at T4; the frequency differs (printed 3/day versus the pool's 1 per encounter), carried as a fork. Price +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. frequency, default the pool row's 1 per encounter governs, with 3/day only if ruled.
