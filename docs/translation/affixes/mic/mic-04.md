## DISPELLING, GREATER (SYNERGY, printed +1; strong abjuration, CL 15th; prereq Dispelling, greater dispel magic) — COVERED
Source: as Dispelling but the target is hit with a targeted greater dispel magic; three times per day.
Pool: Dispelling Strike (Condition 85-90), CL 3/5/8/12/16; the T1 rung (CL 16) is the closest to the printed CL 15. Greater dispel magic raises the check cap, which the pool's CL ladder already models.
Verdict: COVERED at T1; frequency (printed 3/day versus 1/encounter) is the same fork as Dispelling. Price: +1 on top of Dispelling.

## DIVINE WRATH (printed +1; strong evocation, CL 13th; prereq searing light, turn undead, good alignment) — NEW
Source: swift (mental); expend a turn undead attempt to imbue the weapon for 1 round; if your next successful attack hits an undead, extra 1d6 per point of Charisma bonus (minimum 1d6).
Pool: Turn Mastery (Skill/Class 43-48) modifies turning damage; nothing delivers turning as weapon damage. Related to DMG Disruption (destroys undead).
3.5e: as printed; this SCALES with Cha bonus and costs a class resource (turn undead use), both stated. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up, +1d per point of Will-based Cha stand-in, Trigger "spend a Turn Undead / True Faith use" (stand-in and costs OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. no cap printed; default cap +5d6 (Cha bonus 5), design intent.

## DOMINEERING (printed +2; moderate necromancy, CL 11th; prereq fear) — PARTIAL
Source: a creature hit becomes shaken for 1 minute, Will DC 16 negates; does not stack with itself or other fear effects (cannot make a shaken creature frightened).
Pool: Terrifying (Condition 25-30): on hit 1/encounter, fear 1d4 rounds, DC 13/15/17/20/24. Delta = every-hit shaken, no daily limit, weaker condition, fixed DC 16.
3.5e: as printed, flat DC 16. GURPS: Affliction (Fright/Fear-lite), Will resistance, every hit (Cyclic not used; percentage OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. fear immunity applies as normal.

## DOOM BURST (printed +2; moderate necromancy, CL 11th; prereq fear) — NEW
Source: on a critical hit, a wave of blackness makes the target shaken for 5 rounds with no save; applies even to creatures immune to crit extra damage; no stacking with itself or other fear.
Pool: none for the crit-trigger shaken. 3.5e: as printed, trigger confirmed crit, no save (flagged), 5 rounds. GURPS: Affliction (Fright-lite), Trigger critical, no resistance roll (percentage OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. no save printed, kept (crit-only, weak condition); fear-immune targets stay immune.

## DRAGONDOOM (printed +1; moderate transmutation, CL 7th; melee; prereq fell the greatest foe) — NEW
Source: swift (command), three times per day, deliver a smite against a Large or larger dragon-type creature; extra damage per size category above Medium: Large +1d6, Huge +2d6, Gargantuan +3d6, Colossal +4d6; declare before the attack roll; a miss or a non-dragon wastes it.
Pool: Banefire is element-subtype only; Hunter's Mark is the closest bonus-vs-target row. 3.5e: as printed, cap +4d6, 3/day. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up by size category (1d6 / 2d6 / 3d6 / 4d6 for Large, Huge, Gargantuan, Colossal; dice read as-is), Limited Use 3/day. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## DRAGONHUNTER (printed +1; moderate transmutation and necromancy, CL 9th; projectile; prereq keen edge, ray of enfeeblement) — NEW
Source: a dragon-type creature hit by a projectile takes 1 point of Strength damage in addition to normal damage; the weapon's critical multiplier is +1 against dragons (Strength damage is not multiplied); effects that expand or confirm critical threats (keen edge, bless weapon) do not function on this weapon.
Pool: Weakening (Condition 37-42: -1/-1/-2/-3/-4 Str for 1d4 rounds, Fort save) is the nearest; this has no save and is permanent-style ability damage on a type. 3.5e: as printed. GURPS: 1 point of ST damage to the target (a lasting ST reduction; recovery rule OPEN) and +1 to the weapon's multiplier class vs dragons. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. conflict with Keen Edge (the printed text forbids it), default the two cannot share a weapon.

## EAGER (printed +1; moderate evocation, CL 9th; melee; prereq cat's grace) — PARTIAL
Source: draw as a free action; +2 initiative; +2 damage on rolls made during a surprise round and the first round of combat.
Pool: Predator's Instinct (Offensive 73-78): +1/+2/+2/+3/+4 initiative, +1 attack first round at T2+. The +2 initiative matches T4-T3. Delta = free-action draw and +2 damage in the opening rounds.
3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Combat Reflexes-style +2 to initiative-equivalent (Predator's Instinct uses Combat Reflexes), Quick Draw for free, +2 damage opening round (OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.

## ENERGY AURA (printed +2; strong evocation, CL 15th; prereq chill metal, flame blade, Melf's acid arrow, shocking grasp) — PARTIAL
Source: standard (command) activation; each hit deals extra 1d6 of an energy type chosen on activation (acid, cold, electricity or fire), harmless to you; stays until you activate again; ammunition.
Pool: Flaming, Freezing, Shocking and Corroding (Elemental pool) each give +1d6 at T4 as a fixed element. Delta = switchable element. The extra +1 over a single element pays for the switch.
3.5e: as printed. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up with Variable Special Effect (the pool's Prismatic and Chaos Element rows reference variable effects; modifier OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: none.

## ENERGY SURGE (SYNERGY, printed +1; faint evocation, CL 5th; melee; prereq Corrosive, Flaming, Frost or Shock) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as the prerequisite weapon; swift (command) on a successful melee attack: a blast of the same energy deals extra 3d6; the prerequisite need not be active; uses per day = 1 + Con bonus; attunement; a weapon can carry it once per prerequisite, each activation triggers one type.
Pool: the four Elemental rows give the base; no on-demand burst. 3.5e: as printed; scales with Con bonus for uses (stated). GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up 3d6 (dice read as-is), Limited Use. Price: +1 on top of the base. Forks: none.

## ENERVATING (printed +2; moderate necromancy, CL 11th; prereq enervation) — NEW
Source: on a critical hit against a living creature, one negative level on the target; if the subject survives it regains the level after 1 hour; the levels never become permanent drain.
Pool: none (Weakening and Exhaustion are narrower). 3.5e: as printed, no save printed (flagged), crit-only, one level per crit (stacks per crit, each recovers after its own hour). The negative level is the Energy Drained condition for 1 hour (engine). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. stacking of repeated crits (default additive, never multiplicative; cap none printed).
