# MIC weapon properties, batch 01 (Acidic Burst to Blindsighted)
Source: Magic Item Compendium (WotC 2007) pp. 29-30, text read from D:\Backup\I-drive\Sourcebooks\_text (OCR) at the engine index line ranges. Drafts, not ratified. Rules per `docs/translation/AFFIX_TRANSLATION_BRIEF.md`.
Shared conventions for every entry below: GURPS chassis is Gadget (Breakable -25%, Can Be Stolen -10% = -35%) unless stated; GURPS point totals are not stated because the per-trait base costs and the Limited Use / Trigger / Accessibility percentages are not in the repo indices (OPEN, never guessed); gp price = printed bonus squared x 2,000 (+1 = 2,000; +2 = 8,000; +3 = 18,000) or the printed flat gp; "ammunition" means projectile weapons confer the property to ammunition as printed.
Pool abbreviations: rows named are the Registry's canonical pool rows (loot-engine affix-families Part 1).

## ACIDIC BURST (SYNERGY, printed +1; strong conjuration, CL 12th; prereq Corrosive) — PARTIAL
Source: functions as a corrosive weapon; on a critical hit adds acid damage by multiplier x2 1d10, x3 2d10, x4 3d10; does not harm the wielder; works even when Corrosive is not activated; ammunition.
Pool: Corroding (Elemental 25-32) is the acid half. Delta = crit rider only (same shape as DMG Icy Burst).
3.5e: on a confirmed crit, extra acid damage 1d10 / 2d10 / 3d10 by multiplier class (not multiplied again). GURPS: Innate Attack (Corrosion), Follow-Up +0%, Trigger critical, dice default 1d / 2d / 3d. Price: +1 on top of Corrosive (printed synergy price).
Forks: GURPS has no crit multiplier, default use the weapon's 3.5e multiplier class.

## AQUAN (printed +2; moderate evocation, CL 7th; prereq water subtype) — PARTIAL
Source: overcomes the damage reduction of any creature with the fire subtype; extra 2d6 vs such creatures; bestows one negative level on any fire-subtype creature that tries to wield it (lasts while held, never becomes level loss, not removable by restoration while held); ammunition.
Pool: Banefire (Elemental 93-96, +2d6 vs a chosen element subtype) gives the 2d6 rider. Delta = the DR bypass and the subtype-wielder negative level. One family with Auran (earth), Ignan (water) and Terran (air), all +2: weapon keyed to the subtype opposed to its element.
3.5e: as printed, 2d6 untyped, not multiplied on a crit. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up 2d with Accessibility "only vs. the opposed subtype" (percentage OPEN); DR-bypass has no GURPS effect beyond the tag; wielder penalty -1 to all rolls while held (design intent, same default as the alignment weapons).
Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: shared with the alignment-weapon rulings (GURPS subtype tag, wielder penalty); Aquan and Banefire on one weapon, default not allowed.

## AQUATIC (printed +2,000 gp; moderate abjuration, CL 7th; prereq freedom of movement) — NEW
Source: the wielder suffers no underwater penalties for using the weapon, as if under freedom of movement.
Pool: none (Waterborn, Utility 43-48, is water breathing and swim speed, not weapon handling).
3.5e: no penalties from fighting underwater with this weapon. GURPS: no underwater combat penalties with this weapon (the specific modifiers are OPEN). Price: printed 2,000 gp flat. Forks: none.

## ARCANE MIGHT (printed +1; strong transmutation, CL 15th; bows, not crossbows) — NEW
Source: swift (mental) activation; sacrifice a prepared arcane spell (or an unused slot for a spontaneous caster) to gain a damage bonus equal to the spell's level on the next damage roll with the bow that turn.
Pool: none (Reservoir stores a spell; Cost Reduction trades cost).
3.5e: as printed; bonus equals sacrificed spell level; one use per swift action. GURPS: the campaign's casting resource is Energy Reserve; sacrifice ER for a damage bonus. The ER-per-spell-level exchange is OPEN (the translator rule 1 ER = about 5 mana is a rough guide, unverified here). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. cap per round (default one swift activation per round, per the swift action).

## AURAN (printed +2; moderate evocation, CL 7th; prereq air subtype) — PARTIAL
As Aquan, keyed to the earth subtype. Same delta, price and forks.

## BANISHING (printed +2; strong abjuration, CL 13th; prereq banishment) — NEW
Source: free-action activation when you strike an extraplanar creature of 26 HD or fewer; Will DC 20 negates or it is banished to its home plane for at least 24 hours; a creature that saves cannot be banished by the same weapon for 24 hours; save DC +2 for each damage-reduction property (alignment or material) the weapon shares with the creature's DR; three times per day; ammunition.
Pool: none (Dispelling Strike is Condition 85-90 and neutralizes magic).
3.5e: as printed; the DC is fixed at 20 (flat), +2 per shared DR property. GURPS: Affliction-style banish with a Will resistance roll, 3/day (Limited Use percentage OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. does the damage-through rule apply (default: the strike must deal damage).

## BERSERKER (printed +1; moderate enchantment, CL 7th; two-handed melee) — NEW
Source: extra 1d8 damage on any successful attack while raging.
Pool: NAME COLLISION. The pool row "Berserker" (Offensive 61-66) is +1/+1/+2/+3/+4 damage with -AC while attacking and needs no rage; this is a different mechanic. Rename on ratification (e.g. keep the printed name only for this entry and rename the pool-adjacent one), or cite as "Berserker (rage)".
3.5e: +1d8 untyped on any hit while raging; requires rage. GURPS: Innate Attack Follow-Up 1d+2 (Hunter's Mark precedent 1d8 = 1d+1) with Accessibility "while berserk" (percentage OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp.
Forks: 1. GURPS rage stand-in (default: while under the Berserk disadvantage or a campaign rage trait).

## BINDING (printed +1; moderate abjuration, CL 10th; prereq dimensional anchor) — NEW
Source: swift (command) activation; the next successful attack before end of turn prevents the target from extradimensional travel for 10 minutes (as dimensional anchor); twice per day; ammunition.
Pool: NAME COLLISION. The pool "Binding" (Condition 09-16) is entangle for 1 round.
3.5e: as printed; no save printed (dimensional anchor allows none to the target beyond SR). GURPS: prevent Warp / Teleport / plane travel for 10 minutes on the target (mechanism; cost OPEN). Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: 1. spell resistance (default: SR applies as dimensional anchor does).

## BLESSED (printed +1; faint transmutation, CL 5th; prereq bless weapon) — NEW
Source: counts as good-aligned for overcoming DR (continuous); three times per day auto-confirm all critical threats against evil foes for 1 round (bless weapon); other threat or confirmation effects (keen edge, vorpal) confer no additional benefit.
Pool: none; shares the good-alignment tag with DMG Holy (see that file for the alignment rulings).
3.5e: as printed. GURPS: no alignment axis; use the 3.5e tag for the DR rule (no GURPS effect) and "criticals vs evil confirm automatically" as a trigger usable 3/day. Price: +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: shared alignment-tag ruling.

## BLINDSIGHTED (printed +2; moderate divination, CL 6th; prereq see invisibility) — NEW
Source: standard (command) activation; blindsight out to 30 ft for 1 minute while wielding; negated by silence; three times per day; the active weapon whispers (Listen DC 10).
Pool: none (Darksight and Truesight give vision modes, not blindsight).
3.5e: as printed. GURPS: the Blindsight-equivalent perception advantage at 30 ft (named trait and cost OPEN) with Limited Use 3/day. Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: none.
