## IMPEDANCE (printed +2; moderate abjuration, CL 11th; prereq antimagic field) — NEW
Source: mimics the impeded magic planar trait: a creature struck has its spellcasting and spell-like abilities impeded for 1d6 rounds; to cast an impeded spell it must pass a Spellcraft, Intelligence or Charisma check (its best) against DC 15 + spell level; on a failure the spell or use is lost.
Pool: Silencing (Condition 31-36: silence 1d4 rounds, Will save) is the nearest; this has no save and a skill check instead. 3.5e: as printed, every hit, fixed formula DC 15 + spell level. GURPS: Affliction on casting (a casting roll penalty or a failure on a Will roll; modifiers OPEN). Price: +2 = 8,000 gp. Forks: 1. repeated hits: default the duration does not stack, it refreshes.

## IMPLACABLE (printed +3; moderate necromancy, CL 9th; prereq vampiric touch) — NEW (collides with Fatal Wound)
Source: when it damages a living creature the wound bleeds: 2 additional damage at the start of each of the wielder's turns for 5 rounds; multiple wounds are cumulative (three hits in a round = 6 per round for 5 rounds); the bleeding stops on a DC 15 Heal check or any effect that restores hit points; while the wound is active, a spell that restores HP on the target needs a DC 15 caster level check; counts as adamantine against aberrations' DR.
Pool: the ratified Fatal Wound family (Registry section 1) is the Registry's stacking bleed: proc 10/15/20%, ticks 1-3 per stack, caps 3/4/5, a fatal state at cap. Implacable is a second, harder stacking bleed (every damaging hit, 2 per stack, uncapped, 5-round expiry per wound).
3.5e: as printed. Stacking is additive and linear (consistent with the stacking doctrine). No cap is printed; flagged. GURPS: Innate Attack (Toxic) Follow-Up, Cyclic (+400% per cycle), no ST linkage; the per-wound five-round expiry is the cycle count (point total OPEN). Bloodless targets (constructs, undead, oozes, elementals) are immune, per the Registry baseline and the printed "living creature". Price: +3 = 18,000 gp.
Rulings (final): 1. cap at 5 stacks, matching the Fatal Wound Unique cap, because an uncapped stack is a death spiral). 2. healing: the printed "any effect that restores hit points stops the bleeding" agrees with the Registry norm, so any magical healing, including the Crusader heal (D2 reversed 2026-10-04), ends Implacable's wounds. 3. overlap with Fatal Wound on one weapon (default not allowed together).

## KNOCKBACK (printed +1; moderate evocation, CL 11th; prereq repulsion) — COVERED
Source: swift (command), three times per day: the next hit before end of turn forces a DC 19 Fortitude save or the target is pushed back 5 feet; no effect if it cannot move back; only creatures of your size or smaller.
Pool: Knockback (Condition 49-54): on hit push 5/5/10/15/20 ft, GURPS Knockback 1/1/2/3/4, Fortitude. The printed 5 ft matches T5/T4.
Verdict: COVERED at T5-T4. Carry the 3/day limit, DC 19 and size restriction on the card. Price +1 = 2,000 gp. Forks: none.
