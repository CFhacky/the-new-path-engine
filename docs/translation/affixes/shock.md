# SHOCK — COVERED

Text read in full from a 3.5 SRD transcription (https://git.aror.org/florian/d20srd, commit fd8366bb20; carries SRD wording plus cl/modifier fields). The DMG OCR route was permission-denied; not read. · printed bonus-equivalent: +1 (no flat gp printed). CL 8, moderate evocation.
Source text (key facts, quoted briefly): "Upon command, a shock weapon is sheathed in crackling electricity. The electricity does not harm the wielder. The effect remains until another command is given. A shock weapon deals an extra 1d6 points of electricity damage on a successful hit. Bows, crossbows, and slings so crafted bestow the electricity energy upon their ammunition."
Existing pool coverage: Shocking (Elemental pool 17-24): lightning dice +1d4/+1d6/+1d8/+2d6/+3d6 by tier, GURPS IA (Burning [Lightning], Follow-Up). Gap: pool T4 (+1d6) equals the printed damage exactly; the SRD adds a command-word on/off toggle and ammunition-bestowing for bows/crossbows/slings, neither stated in the pool row (the pool also adds "ignore metal armor DR" at T2+, which the SRD does not have).
Verdict: no new family. A Shock-equivalent item is Shocking at T4 (printed +1 = 2,000 gp if the item is priced from the SRD figure). Optional rider, not a new row: command-word toggle and ammunition rule.
