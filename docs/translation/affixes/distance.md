Translation identity: flat (range increment x2; wielder Str and enhancement never scale it) · armor-agnostic (changes range only; no damage or armor effect) · no cap (passive, no stacking, no proc d100).
3.5e: Ranged weapons only. The weapon's range increment is doubled (a 100-ft bow is 200 ft). Range penalty per increment is unchanged; the maximum number of increments is the weapon's normal one (PHB rule, not in the fetched ability text, so treat the maximum range as scaling with the doubled increment). No save, no SR, no DC, no action. No Fatal Wound interaction.
GURPS: Chassis: the launcher is the Gadget (Breakable DR 6 -25%, Can Be Stolen -10% = -35%). Mechanism: the weapon's 1/2D and Max are doubled. The nearest printed trait is the Increased Range enhancement, +10%/level in gurps_trait_index, but the index does not print what one level multiplies (whether one level doubles range is not verified); so the level count and total are OPEN. Magical -10% not applied (no attack). No d100.
Pricing: +1 bonus-equivalent = 2,000 gp (bonus-squared x 2,000; matches printed +1).
Name collision: SRD Distance (identical); SRD Throwing (melee gains a 10 ft increment) and Seeking (ranged only), both queued; spell clairaudience/clairvoyance (prerequisite only); aspect Siege (24E).
Forks needing a ruling:
