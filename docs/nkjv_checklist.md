# NKJV check: scripture wording in the readings companion

Compare each line with your NKJV. Where one differs, reply with the reference and the correct wording, or simply "all good". Spelling, capitals and punctuation matter. The NKJV capitalises divine pronouns (He, Me, You) and prints LORD in small capitals. In poetry, each line starts with a capital.

The shorter fragments in the companion ("of good and evil", "the gate of heaven", "absent from the body", "present with the Lord", "all the kingdoms of the world and their glory", "into the outer darkness", "I knew you to be a hard man") are cut from the same verses and will be corrected along with them.
