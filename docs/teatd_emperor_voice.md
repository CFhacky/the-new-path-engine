# The Emperor's voice: a catalogue

*Every way the Emperor of Mankind communicates in Dan Abnett's* The End and the Death, *verse by verse. This is the evidence for reading 17 of the companion (`docs/teatd_readings.md`).*

## How this was made

All three volumes were read in full, and every passage in which the Emperor communicates, or in which someone comments on how he does, was recorded.

- **Quotations** are verbatim. Every one has been checked by script against the verse it is cited to, using the address maps in `reference/addresses/`. The one exception is Abnett's afterword, which sits after the last verse and is labelled "Afterword".
- **Notes** are interpretive. They say what each passage does. They are readings, not facts.
- **Telepathic speech** is set between plus signs, *+like this+*. But Malcador, Actae and the Dark Angels' Librarius use the same convention, so every plus-sign line was assigned to its speaker from context. Each volume opens with a list of the plus-sign speech that is *not* the Emperor.

## The modes

| Mode | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DIRECT** | The Emperor in his own voice, telepathic or aloud. |
| **MOUTHPIECE** | Someone else speaks his words or will: Malcador, the Hetaeron (above all Caecaltus Dusk), a relay of messengers. |
| **ASPECT** | He appears as, or speaks as, someone or something else: the war-king, the Dark King, the wolf, the seducer, the false Loken, the empty husks. |
| **VISION** | A scene, memory or sight he puts into someone's mind. The vision is itself the message. |
| **SILENCE** | He pointedly does not answer, or someone remarks on his silence or unknowability. |
| **SIGN** | Communication without words: a look, a gesture, a star, a war-cry, a card, power lent through a blade, an act. |
| **COMMENT** | Narration or dialogue about how he speaks, or doesn't. |

Signs and silences far outnumber his own words. Where he speaks directly:

- **Volume I:** most often, and mostly to Malcador. After Malcador takes the Throne, he speaks to his sons in person (2:xviii onward).
- **Volume II:** there is no plus-sign speech from him at all. All ten DIRECT rows are the one call, "Those who may hear me, join me now" (8:xvi), recorded once for each listener who hears it.
- **Volume III:** he speaks at only three moments: orders over Sanguinius's body (9:v), his words to the Old Four at the parley (9:ix), and the forgiveness (10:xvi). There is also one unspoken thought that Horus reads in him (10:viii).

## Volume I

**Plus-sign speech in Volume I that is not the Emperor:**
- **Malcador:** 1:v, 1:xi, 2:xii and 2:xiv. From the Throne, Malcador sends the whispers and names heard by Zahariel, Sindermann and Mauer, Keeler and Loken (3:x–4:vii). He also narrates 3:x and 3:xxxii.
- **Horus:** the vision in 3:x of the Emperor cut down is shown to Malcador by Horus.
- **Actae:** Katt speaks for Actae at 3:xx ("using the girl as a mouthpiece").
- **Others:** Typhon's offer (3:xv); Lord Cypher's projected voice (3:xxvii).
